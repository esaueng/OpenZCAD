Section view slice (schema v45, [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#implementation-decisions-d03-section-views-schema-v45)): a section line dragged across a front, top or side view (snapped to the view's middle or a circle's centre) makes a section view, moved into place along its arrows and kept aligned with its source on the projection angle's side. `DrawingView.section` stores only the source, a label and the plane's depth. Each body is cut by an exact half-space boolean in the geometry worker and projected like any view, so the cut outline is kernel-sampled exact edges; cut faces are hatched, hidden lines follow the view's toggle, and the cutting-plane line (chain, thick ends, arrows, letters) and "SECTION A-A" caption go through the one page model to preview, PDF and SVG. Where the pinned kernel refuses the exact cut (most planes through a cylindrical hole in a box), the body is cut from its tessellation and labelled approximate, without dimensionable cut edges; a plane that cuts nothing is refused on the view and blocks export. Sections follow the sheet's explicit Update and reopen from the saved document. D02 dimensions resolve on the cut body's own edges: a blind Ø8 bore 18 deep, cut down its axis, dimensions its floor at 8 and its depth at 18, and a moved floor breaks the dimension rather than rebinding it. Evidence: `test/drawing-sections.test.ts` (schema refusals, commands, exact cut of the bored cylinder with hatch area 456 mm², floor and depth dimensions, broken reference, refused plane, tessellation fallback on the bored plate), `DrawingWorkspace.test.tsx` (hatched section with line and caption, refusal without retry, drag-and-click creation) and `test/e2e/drawing-sections.spec.ts` (bored plate: section across the bore, Ø12 dimension on the section's circle, SVG/PDF contents, reload). Open: detail views, free and stepped section planes, lineage for cut edges, exact cuts the kernel refuses.

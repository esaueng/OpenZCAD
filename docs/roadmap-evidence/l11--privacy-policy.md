Phase 0 prerequisite: a privacy policy page, the precondition ADR-024 sets for any public end-to-end encryption claim. `apps/web/public/privacy.html` is a static page (no script, nothing from another origin, no sign-in, nothing added to the app bundle) served at `/privacy` by Cloudflare's default asset HTML handling, and linked from Settings › Privacy & data and the email sign-in form (`privacyPolicyHref`: the same-origin path in browsers, `https://zcad.app/privacy` in the desktop app). Every statement was checked against the code: accounts (email, derived account ID, hashed 10-minute codes, hashed 30-day session cookie `__Host-openzcad_session`, Turnstile with the client IP, keyed-hash rate limits), standard projects readable by the server versus encrypted projects (opt-in per account during the beta, possibly not yet available; what is encrypted; ADR-024's plaintext metadata, including signer account and device per version, device lists and contacts), keys and recovery (non-extractable browser keys, macOS Keychain on desktop, no server-held recovery key, lost keys mean lost cloud data, local copies remain), standard and encrypted share links (fragment keys never reach the server), collaboration rooms, the assistant (the exact request inventory from `consentCopy.ts`, the configured provider named as today's, per-project consent on encrypted projects), Glimpse analytics levels, Cloudflare email, processors, retention, deletion routes and the desktop app. Operator Esau Engineering LLC, contact help@zcad.app and Michigan governing law come from the maintainer; the effective date is the one placeholder left, and `apps/web/src/lib/privacyPolicyPlaceholders.test.ts` ("FILL IN BEFORE MERGE") fails while any bracketed placeholder remains, so the page cannot ship unfinished.

Measure workbench redesign: the six up-front modes give way to a pick
session. One pick reads as itself; two picks read every way they relate
(parallel, central, distance, maximum, angle), the most specific first and
each figure once, and Keep (or Enter) records the reading shown. Units,
precision, Ø/R and the Distance point kind sit behind one settings menu
(`apps/web/src/lib/measureReadings.ts`,
`useMeasurementWorkbench.test.tsx`, `MeasurementDock.test.tsx`,
`test/e2e/measure.spec.ts`).

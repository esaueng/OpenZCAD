2026-11-05 widened combination campaign (WS1). `test/support/combination-matrix.ts` now generates cells from data, with stable ids. It covers seven operand pairs, nine placements and three scales; union, subtract and intersect in both orders; seam and unrelated-edge fillets and chamfers and a cap offset; and ten follow-ups. Each cell is checked against independent oracles (`test/support/combination-oracle.ts`: closed forms, inclusion–exclusion with chord-integrated overlap, Pappus and prism blend sections), against replay determinism (undo/redo, reopen), against route equivalence (direct and source resize, removing a blend and deleting the fillet), and against a STEP round trip. A cell lands in one of five states: qualified, typed refusal, untyped refusal, wrong or timeout. A shrinker (`K10_SHRINK=1`) and a minimal-repro printer cover failing cells. On the `2026.1.122` pin, the 1,392 cells are 725 qualified, 399 typed refusals, 216 untyped refusals and 94 wrong. The CI slice (`test/combination-matrix.test.ts`, 42 cells, `combination-matrix.ledger.json`) stays in the smoke list. The full campaign (`test/combination-matrix.full.test.ts`, 1,350 cells, `combination-matrix.full.ledger.json`) runs in the full suite. Wrong cells: a box minus a half-embedded torus returns the box unchanged; a direct wall resize under a seam blend loses the blend volume; an internally tangent cross drill is 2.2 mm³ high; and the Steinmetz cross drill is 0.071 mm³ off.

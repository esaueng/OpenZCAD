Wrap, emboss and engrave (schema v111): a `wrap` feature wraps regions of a sketch drawn on a plane parallel to the axis of a cylindrical or conical face onto that face, preserving arc length, and embosses them out of it or engraves them into it by `depth`, turned about the axis by `rotationDeg` and slid along it by `axialOffset`; the result is one body. Out of the face is away from its material, so in a bore an emboss stands toward the axis and an engrave cuts into the wall. The kernel's `wrapProfileOnCylinder` (Remus) builds the wrapped material as the solid between two radii: exact cylinders inside and out, exact planes for edges along or across the axis, and for every other edge (an oblique line wraps to a helix, an arc or spline to a curve with no rational form) an exact ruled wall between certified cubic fits whose distance from the exact wrapped curve is proven within 1e-7 of the wrap's scale by the Hermite remainder. The emboss reaches a quarter of its depth into the cylinder and the engrave a quarter out of it, so the union or cut meets the face across a margin rather than a coincident surface. On a cone, the kernel's `wrapProfileOnCone` reads the regions in the cone's development (arc length across, length along the generator from the circle at the sketch's origin) and builds the solid between two normal offsets: exact cones inside and out (a cone offset along its normal is a cone), every edge a certified fit from a closed-form fourth-derivative bound of the development map, and walls ruled along the normal between an edge's two equal-span fits; an annular sector of the development lands within 1e-6 of its closed form. Arc length is read along the sketch plane itself, so Turn moves a wrap round the axis without scaling it and a region lands under where it is drawn whichever way the sketch faces. Refused by name: a sketch not parallel to the axis, a face that is neither a cylinder nor a cone, a depth past the axis or an offset past a cone's apex, a region reaching the apex or spanning a full turn, a rational curve other than a circle or ellipse. Known limit: Remus reads the volume of a cone or cylinder face trimmed by a curved boundary to its face integrator's boundary chording (a wrapped disc 1.3e-4 low on a cone, 4e-4 high engraved in a radius-10 post, which exact mass properties do not show), so a body wrapped with curved regions reports a volume carrying that error. Wrap runs in place (Edges & faces): regions are clicks, the cylinder or cone a face click, depth an arrow out of (or into) the face, turn a ring about the axis, offset an arrow along it; it reopens from History. Evidence: `test/wrap.test.ts` (the v111 round trip and older-schema refusal; a rectangle embossed at the post's volume + A·(r2² − r1²)/2R; a disc engraved; a quarter turn and offset; a sketch not parallel to the axis refused; a label embossed toward the axis and engraved into the wall of a bore at the closed-form volumes; a label embossed and engraved on a frustum through its development, one body each with exact cone faces, within the integrator's chording of the developed volume) and `test/e2e/wrap.spec.ts` (region and face clicks, the depth dragged, one body at the closed-form volume).

Sheet images (schema v92, built on v91; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#sheet-images-d02-schema-v92)), completing the row's notes and sheet images: the Image tool (I) places one of the project's reference images (I05) on the paper by its id, so its pixels stay in the content-addressed store and travel as reference images do; Properties chooses the picture and its printed width, and its height keeps the picture's shape. A picture deleted from the project breaks the image by name (`image-missing`) rather than rebinding. Pictures are drawn beneath the lines into the preview, embedded in SVG as data URLs and in PDF as DCT image objects (a JPEG's bytes as they are, a PNG re-encoded on white), and left out of DXF R12 with a status note; a sheet without one exports byte-identical. A content gate refuses them below v92. Evidence: `test/drawing-sheet-images.test.ts` (validation by name, the printed size from the picture's aspect, centred placement and box, a page without pictures unchanged and one off the paper dropped, the `image-missing` break, the tool's draft, the JPEG frame reader, SVG embedding and its refusal without the bytes, the PDF image object once per picture with its placement, DXF leaving it out, the v92 gate) and `test/e2e/drawing-sheet-images.spec.ts` (a PNG inserted in Build, placed on a sheet, resized, exported to SVG and PDF with its pixels, and reopened).

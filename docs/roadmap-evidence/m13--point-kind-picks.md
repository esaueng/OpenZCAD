Viewport picks that choose a point kind (no schema or kernel change; measurements stay out of the document). In Distance, the Measure dock gains "Point to pick": Auto, Endpoint, Midpoint, Center, Centroid. Auto keeps the existing derivation. A chosen kind records that notable point of each pick through `notablePointTarget`, by identity: an endpoint is the end the snap glyph named, else the end nearer the pick, stored as the edge's start or end; a midpoint lies on the trimmed line or arc; a center is a circle's, an arc's or a cylinder or hole axis's; a centroid is a face's area centroid. A pick without that kind is refused with the reason ("That edge is not a circle or arc, so it has no center.") rather than recorded as another kind. The viewport's measure snaps are scoped to the chosen kind (`measureSnapKinds`), so the glyph only ever names what the click will record; the kinds live in a small module so the workspace scopes snaps without loading the deferred measurement library. Evidence: `test/notable-point-measure.test.ts` ("point-kind picks": nearer and snap-named ends, line and arc midpoints, arc and hole centres, an area centroid, four refusals with reasons, snap scoping, an exact midpoint-to-hole-centre distance), `MeasurementDock.test.tsx` and the Chromium smoke case "a Distance pick asked for midpoints records each edge midpoint" in `test/e2e/measure.spec.ts`.

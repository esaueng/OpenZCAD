Split plane through two edges (with R01, slice 1b; owner decision D2, a fixed numeric plane): with the body picked, Split takes two straight edges, on the split body or another, in place of a face or a principal or datum plane. The pair resolves once at pick time (`edgePairPlane` in `packages/geometry`) into Split's existing numeric plane, so there is no new plane kind, no link to the edges and no schema change, and editing the edges later does not move the split. The edges are put in a canonical order first, so either pick order and either edge direction give a bit-identical plane. Its origin is the centroid of the four ends; its normal is canonical (first component beyond the direction quantum positive), and a negative Offset slides the plane the other way. Coplanar crossing or parallel pairs resolve; pairs whose directions differ by at most the linear tolerance over the longer edge and lie on one line (`collinear`), pairs with an end farther than the linear tolerance from the fitted plane (`skew`) and curved edges (`curved-edge`) are refused before any rebuild with their own sentences after "Plane:", with the edges kept to re-pick.

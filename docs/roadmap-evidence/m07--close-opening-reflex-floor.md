Pushing a window's floor across the opening now also closes a window whose
floor is not convex, such as an L- or T-shaped floor where the window is cut
across a rib or rail band that thickens the wall. Between sides parallel to
the push, the fill is built as the union of convex pieces of the floor. Each
piece is bounded by the floor's own exact side planes, and the result is the
same as a push on a rectangular floor. Leaning sides or blends beside a
reflex corner still refuse (`test/close-opening-direct-edit.test.ts`).

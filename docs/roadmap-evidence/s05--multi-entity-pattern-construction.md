Multi-entity pattern and construction selections. Linear and Circular pattern armed over a selection (box, Shift/Cmd-click or Cmd/Ctrl+A), like Mirror, ask only for the direction line or the pivot, then the same values and settings, and repeat every selected entity in one undoable transaction. Each entity is planned as the single-entity pattern would plan it, against the sketch with the instances already planned for the entities before it, so no entity's instances land on another's; an entity the pattern cannot repeat is left out and counted, the direction line is skipped if selected, and only a selection with nothing repeatable is refused. The entity editor's Construction toggle acts on the whole selection when several entities are selected (labelled with the count): all become construction unless every one already is, then all become regular again, in one undo step. No schema change. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/modify.test.ts` (a selection repeated by extent with its links, sequential planning and counted refusals, a uniform circular selection, a mixed construction selection both ways and undo, the selection flow from pick to transaction), `machine.test.ts`, `SketchEntityEditor.test.tsx` and `test/e2e/sketch-modify-tools.spec.ts` ("a pattern and Construction act on a box selection in one step each").

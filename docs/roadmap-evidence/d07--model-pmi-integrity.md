Model PMI integrity (schema v157, built on v155; v156 is claimed by #2425): a form tolerance stays on a face it can control. Flatness needs a plane, cylindricity a cylinder, circularity a surface of revolution and straightness a plane, cylinder or cone (`modelPmiSurfaceError`). Objects refuses "Move to selected face" onto any other face and disables those characteristics for the frame's face. A stored frame on such a face shows broken (`surface-incompatible`), and AP242 leaves it out instead of writing it as semantic PMI. Renaming a datum feature carries every frame that names it in one undoable `pmi.update` (`followReferences`); an older log replays as recorded. An undeclared-datum frame names its slot and asks for that datum, not a face re-pick. Every model PMI entry an AP242 export leaves out (an incompatible face, an undeclared or unwritten datum, a datum Objects folders put on another part, a broken face) is named in the export's status and the activity card. Evidence: `test/model-pmi-integrity.test.ts` (cylindricity written on a bore and left out with a warning on its flat cap; a rename of C to F in two frames' secondary slots, one with a modifier, exported with ordered datum systems, undone in one step and replayed; a v155 document renamed after opening; the v156 gate; the slot guidance; omissions for an undeclared datum, a cross-part datum, an unwritten datum and a stale face), `PmiRows.test.tsx`, and `test/e2e/model-pmi.spec.ts` and `test/e2e/step-pmi.spec.ts` (a rename followed by its frame and undone; a deleted datum's frame named at export).

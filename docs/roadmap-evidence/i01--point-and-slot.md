Delivered: point and slot sketch entities (schema v59; v58 is claimed by the M16 sheet-metal change). Stored documents are never rewritten, and an older client refuses a v59 document rather than dropping either entity. A `point` sketch object (`x`, `y`) is one solver point named `center`. Coincident, midpoint, distance, symmetric, point-on-curve, lock and disconnect all take it; curve-only relations refuse it by name. It bounds no profile, splits no curve, is left out of sketch DXF/SVG output like construction geometry, draws as a dot, is selected by a click and edits X/Y on the entity card. A `slot` is a new S04 composite kind (`center-to-center`): side 0 a line, side 1 the half circle about the second centre, side 2 the opposite line, side 3 the half circle about the first centre, counter-clockwise, plus a construction centre line as its one helper. Its relations are four corner joins, four line-to-arc tangents at the joins, equal ends and the centre line held on both centres. They leave exactly position, rotation, centre distance and radius free with nothing redundant, and a radius on an end or a distance on the centre line drives it. The composite resolves only while its arcs are equal half circles, its lines run tangent at the corners and the centre line joins the centres; otherwise it reads as broken. Because its members are ordinary lines and arcs, region analysis, profiles, the exact build, the solver and undo need no new path. Rail: Slot and Point are tiles on the shape strip beside Polygon (Slot: both end centres, then a click off the axis for the radius, with a live outline; Point: one click, which snaps). Evidence: `test/sketch-point-slot.test.ts` (pinned kernel: slot rank equals equations at 5 DOF, radius and centre-distance dimensions drive it, the stadium region has the exact 2rL + πr² area and extrudes to it times the height, one undo removes the whole slot, a record with the wrong side kinds is refused, a point fully defined by a midpoint relation solves to the midpoint with no region, curve-only relations refused, schema v59 required), `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/composites.test.ts`, `SketchToolRail.test.tsx`, and `test/e2e/sketch-point-slot.spec.ts` (three clicks draw a slot as line, arc, line, arc and a centre line under one composite with 6 coincident, 4 tangent and 1 equal relations; one click places a point snapped on the centre line's midpoint; undo removes each in one step).

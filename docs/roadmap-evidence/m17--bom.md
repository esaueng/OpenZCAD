Slice 3, standard parts in the AS03 bill of materials (no schema change). The exact build records each standard part as it placed it (`DerivedState.standardParts`): the length of every part, one per bore of a hole it is in, a hole-matched length included. `deriveAssemblyBom` lists a standard part by its catalogue designation ("ISO 4762 M6×1 × 20 socket head cap screw", "ASME B18.2.2 1/4-20 UNC hex nut"), not its body's name. Parts of one designation share one row whichever feature placed them, and a rigid copy keeps its source's row. A part in a hole counts once per bore, while a scaled or edited part becomes its own part. Parts whose bores came out at different lengths are named "(mixed lengths)". The CSV keeps its columns, and its Part cell carries the designation. Evidence: `test/standard-parts-bom.test.ts` (grouping by designation across features, quantities, CSV, a rigid copy, ANSI parts named in inches, and on the exact kernel one screw per bore of a two-point clearance hole at its matched 20 mm counted twice), and `test/e2e/standard-parts-bom.spec.ts`. That spec places an ISO M6 screw with the Standard part tool by click and length drag in a block that is already an assembly. It adds the screw from Assemble's Not in the assembly list, then checks the BOM row and the CSV name it by its designation at the dragged length.

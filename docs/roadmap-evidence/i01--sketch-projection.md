Delivered: sketch projection with a linked/unlinked choice (research-audit Stage 2d, ZC-013; schema v33). An additive `projection` record on line, arc, circle and ellipse objects stores the source body and edge by schema-v5 lineage plus `linked` or `unlinked`; stored documents are never rewritten. The sketch rail's Project tool (U; its strip picks Linked or Unlinked) projects a picked model edge, or every boundary edge of a picked face, along the sketch normal. It follows the Construction toggle like every other drawing tool. Straight edges become lines. Circles and arcs become circles and arcs when parallel to the sketch, and exact ellipses and elliptical arcs when tilted. A line along the normal, a circle seen edge-on and any other curve kind are refused by name. Linked: at the sketch's history position the exact build re-finds each edge through verified lineage only (no hash match, no proximity). Every later feature in that build reads the re-projected geometry, so a downstream extrude follows an upstream resize in the same rebuild. The state is published as `derived.sketchProjections` (current, moved or broken). A move is written back to the object as a normalization with no undo step of its own. Line ends keep their names when an edge comes back reversed. The solver holds linked objects fixed, and drag, trim, fillet and chamfer refuse them until they are unlinked. Broken (source deleted, unavailable at the sketch's place in the history, or no longer the same curve kind): the stored geometry, which is the last valid projection, keeps building, and the sketch carries an advisory. Repair is an explicit Re-pick source on the entity card. It is validated by a rebuild before it commits and keeps the object id. Unlink instead keeps the geometry as an independent copy. A new linked pick is refused when its edge does not exist at the sketch's place in the history. Unlinked: copied once, ordinary editable geometry, source kept as provenance. U06: the Objects sketch row counts its projections and says when one is broken. Each projected entity is listed under it with its source feature and its linked/independent/broken role. The History row of a sketch with a broken projection is flagged. Evidence: `test/sketch-projection.test.ts` (exact kernel: a pick re-projects to exactly what it stored; a linked outline follows w 20→32 with the extrude at 32·10·3 in the same rebuild, then settles current after write-back; reversed line ends; unlinked never moves; a deleted source is broken with the last valid 25·10·3 plate still built and four advisories; an orphaned lineage refuses; history-position refusal; exact rim circle; v33 round trip and malformed-record refusals), `test/sketch-projection-geometry.test.ts` (closed-form line, circle, arc-side, tilted-circle r·cos θ ellipse and elliptical-arc oracles, refusals, tolerance and end-order rules), `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/projection.test.ts`, `ObjectsPanel.projections.test.tsx`, `HistoryTimeline.test.tsx`, `SketchEntityEditor.test.tsx`, `SketchToolRail.test.tsx`, `machine.test.ts`, and `test/e2e/sketch-projection.spec.ts` (projects a box's top face linked, widens the box from History and sees the stored outline follow, deletes the box and sees broken in Objects, History and the card, then unlinks).
Remaining: silhouette projection of curved faces, spline and ellipse edge projection, and offsetting a body edge chain into a sketch.

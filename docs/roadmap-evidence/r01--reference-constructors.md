Reference constructors (schema v108), completing the target catalogue with the points and systems of schema v99. A `normal-to-edge` datum plane stands square to a model edge, its normal the edge's tangent from end 0 toward end 1: at a fraction along the edge (of the length on a line or arc, of the parameter span on any other curve; the kernel evaluates the edge exactly there, and an arc gives the plane's u toward its centre), which covers a plane on a curve at a point, or through a point when the edge is straight, a plane perpendicular to an edge at a point. An `edge-angle` plane runs through a straight edge, turned by an angle about it from the plane through the edge parallel to a reference plane source. A `face-normal` datum axis stands square to any face where a point drops onto it: the kernel carries the point to the nearest point of the face's surface and evaluates the outward normal there. Edges and faces are held by verified lineage only. Refused by name, with nothing published: a closed edge, a fraction outside 0 to 1, a point or an angle on a curved edge, an edge not parallel to the reference plane, a foot that falls off the face, and a face with no normal there. A plane whose edge was lost, or an axis whose face was, offers Re-pick source edge or face from History; only the source changes. In place, the Plane tool takes an edge clicked away from its ends (its position dragged along the edge), the edge and a point, or a straight edge and a plane (its angle turned on a ring); the Axis tool takes a face and an edge end, or a curved face at the click. Both reopen in their cards. Evidence: `test/datum-reference-constructors.test.ts` (the v108 round trip and v107 refusal; a plane at a quarter of an upright edge splitting a block 5 and 15 deep, an edit to a half moving the split, and a plane through a typed point; a plane at the middle of an arc with its normal the tangent and u toward the centre, and a point on that arc refused; planes through a top edge at 0 and 45° from XY, and an upright edge refused as not parallel; axes square to a block's top, a cylinder's side and a top corner, and a foot off the face refused; a plane on a deleted block's edge re-attached to another block's edge) and `test/e2e/datum-reference-constructors.spec.ts` (an upright edge picked and the position dragged, a top edge and the top face picked and the ring turned, and the top face and a corner picked for an axis, each checked where the exact build drew it).

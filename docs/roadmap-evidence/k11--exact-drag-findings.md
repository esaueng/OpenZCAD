2026-11-21 findings (owner keeps commit checks blocking and exact). A flagged prototype (`h02/exact-drag-streaming`, unmerged) streams exact face-offset previews under the drag proxy and builds blends display-only from the first changed feature. On the RX 6600 it reaches first exact geometry at 95–103 ms on the native holder and 174–215 ms on the filleted-union cases (from 2.3–2.7 s), but regresses the filleted-union width release from 98 to 330 ms, because the release then pays the fillet's blocking checks. Profiling those checks on a native Remus reproduction (Remus branch `perf/k11-blend-guards`, `crates/operations/tests/blend_guard_filleted_union.rs`) found no exact 2× speedup. The material guard (~91 ms) is spread over NURBS-band ray crossings (~36 ms, mostly certified subdivision), ray–torus tests (~23 ms) and BVH bookkeeping (~17 ms). The volume guard (~86 ms) is cylinder Green's-theorem integrals that are genuine cache misses. Backlog of exact savings (about 20–30 ms in all): reuse per-surface extraction operators (2–3 ms); a proven torus early-out (≤ 20 ms; needs the solver's tangent-ray behaviour worked out); a first-derivative-only NURBS path for the cylinder integral (5–8% of the volume guard). Next app steps if resumed: prepare the drag before the first move (cold first preview ~200 ms), and keep a checked fillet checkpoint for the release.

Phase 5, slice 5-3a, invitations and KeyPackages for encrypted projects, Worker and crypto only (the browser flow is slice 5-3b), behind `E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED`, `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED` and the account's project-sharing rollout, with migration 0038 `e2ee_invitations`, `e2ee_key_packages` and `e2ee_welcomes`. `packages/crypto/src/invitations.ts` adds the owner-signed invitation statement ("OZI" v1, documented in the package README): project, invitation id, owner, a labelled SHA-256 of the invitee's normalized email, role, at most seven days of validity, and the signing device's id and key hash, signed by that certified device with its own label; `verifyProjectInvitation` refuses non-canonical bytes, another signing key, a forged signature, an expired or future statement and another context with typed reasons. `apps/web/worker/e2eeInvitations.ts` serves `POST`/`GET /api/e2ee/projects/:id/invitations` and `DELETE …/invitations/:id` (owner only; the statement is verified against the owner's newest device list, the plaintext route's recipient rules and invitation budget apply, only the email hash is stored, and the email names the sender, never the project, with an `#encrypted-invite=` link), `POST /api/e2ee/invitations/accept` (binds the signed-in account whose email hashes to the statement's; no membership yet) and `GET /api/e2ee/invitations`, `POST …/key-packages` (the accepted invitee's certified devices, one KeyPackage and leaf per project and device, republishing replaces an unconsumed one), `POST …/key-packages/claim` (owner; one statement claims every unclaimed KeyPackage of the invitation, and a claimed one is never handed out again, also enforced by a trigger) and `GET …/welcome?deviceId=`. The Add form of `POST …/mls/commits` states the invitation, the claimed KeyPackages and the Welcome: the Worker refuses one that does not name exactly the owner's claimed, unconsumed KeyPackages (`E2EE_ADD_MISMATCH`), and the same conditions gate the Commit row inside the batch, which consumes the KeyPackages, registers their leaves, stores the Welcome per recipient KeyPackage, inserts the `project_members` row with the invitation's role, completes the invitation and logs the access event. Accepted encrypted invitations relate owner and invitee for device-list lookups (M2; a pending one relates nobody, since the server holds only the email hash); project purge and account erasure clear the new tables after a cached probe. The plaintext invitation routes are unchanged and keep refusing encrypted projects; an encrypted invitation's token is unknown to them. Ordinary traffic does not depend on 0038; the flags stay off and the migration needs its own authorization before it reaches production.

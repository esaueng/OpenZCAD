Datum points and named coordinate systems (schema v95). Document: `datum-point` features place a point at an offset from a typed position, a model vertex or an earlier point, at a straight edge's midpoint, at a circle's or arc's centre (both by lineage), or where an earlier datum axis pierces a plane source. `datum-csys` features place a right-handed system through three points (origin, X, XY plane), on a plane source's own frame (origin moved to a point's foot), or at a point turned about world X, Y and Z. Consumers hold them by id: every point source (three-point and parallel planes, two-point axes, systems, later points) takes `{ type: 'datum-point' }`; a system's XY, YZ and ZX planes are datum plane sources (`{ type: 'datum-csys', plane }`), so sketches, Split and Mirror reach them through a plane; a `csys-axis` datum axis runs along its X, Y or Z for revolves, circular patterns and Moves; and a Move's `csysAlign` carries a body from one system (or the world) onto another. Each resolves once at its history position and is published in `derived.datumPoints` / `derived.datumCsys`. Refused by name, with nothing published: a curved edge's midpoint, a straight edge's centre, an axis parallel to its plane, coincident or collinear system points, and any consumer of a point or system that failed, is suppressed or comes later. Deleting a point or system something is built on is refused with its dependents named; edits walk their dependents. In place, Point (Create fold) drags X, Y and Z arrows from the world origin or a picked edge end, or takes a picked edge's midpoint or an arc's centre; Coordinate system drags its origin and turns its rings, or takes three picked points or a plane; both reopen from History. The viewport draws points as crosses and systems as X/Y/Z triads; the Plane and Axis cards offer earlier points, and the Axis card a System axis mode. Evidence: `test/datum-point-csys.test.ts` (the v95 round trip and v91 refusal, reference rules, delete refusals, typed and chained points moving every plane, axis and system built on them, edge midpoint and arc centre by lineage, an axis piercing a plane and the parallel refusal, a system's planes, axis and an aligned Move landing a block at the closed-form box, a failed system refusing its axis) and `test/e2e/datum-points-csys.spec.ts` (a point dragged out along X, a midpoint picked on an edge, a system dragged and placed, the first point reopened and dragged along Y). Open: planes on a curve, perpendicular to an edge and through an edge at an angle; the axis perpendicular to a face at a point.

Slice 4, cosmetic threads and callout metadata for D02 (schema v67, up from v66; no kernel change). `tapped.cosmetic: true` describes a tapped hole's thread without cutting it: the bore stays its tap drill and keeps its `hole.face.bore` name, and a cosmetic thread reads no print clearance and raises no print advice. Callout metadata: `HoleCallout` (shared) is what any reader of a hole gets: the count of bores, the bore's diameter (the table's for a standard), its depth or through, the style, the standard, and the thread (`M6×1` or `1/4-20 UNC`, major diameter and pitch in document units, length or the whole bore, hand, cosmetic or cut). document-core's `holeCallout(document, featureId)` reads it from the stored hole as the build does, and `holeOfBoreFace` names the hole a face is a bore of from its lineage: directly, on sketch points (`hole.face.at.<point>.bore`) or as an M02 pattern copy (`pattern.face.instance.<n>.source.<hole>.…`). `holeCalloutLabel` writes it as a drawing does, numbers in the drawing's precision: `M6×1 THRU`, `3× M6×1 LH × 8 DEEP, Ø5 × 12 DEEP`, `Ø6.6 THRU, M6 CLEARANCE`, `Ø5 × 12 DEEP, M6×1 TAP DRILL`. D02 reads it: a diameter dimension on the rim of a hole's bore, whose hole has a standard or a thread and whose rim is the bore's own diameter, is labelled with the callout instead of the bare diameter, on screen and in PDF/SVG export (`resolveSheetAnnotations` takes the drawing workspace's lookup). A content gate refuses `cosmetic` in a document that declares v66 or older.

UI: a tapped hole's Options gain Thread form (Modeled, Cosmetic); a cosmetic thread keeps its draggable length and drops the fit. History reads "M6×1 tapped (cosmetic) × 10 mm".

Evidence: `test/hole-callouts.test.ts` (14 cases: the callout text for plain, clearance, tap-drill, through, whole-blind, stopped-short left-hand three-point and UNC holes; bore-face naming directly, on points and as a pattern copy; `holeCallout` reading the table's diameter over a stored one, the thread and its form and the point count; the v67 gate and the print-clearance read; on the pinned kernel a cosmetic M6 hole whose volume is the plain tap drill's, closed form, with no advice, and a D02 diameter on its rim in the top view reading `M6×1 THRU`, `M6×1 × 6 DEEP, Ø5 × 8 DEEP` and `Ø5 THRU, M6×1 TAP DRILL`, and `Ø5` without the lookup; a feature pattern leaving a cosmetic thread's copies plain tap drills); `apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/tools.test.ts` and `edit.test.ts`; `test/e2e/hole-callouts.spec.ts`, which clicks the block's top face, chooses a tapped M6 with a cosmetic thread, drags the thread's arrow short of the blind bore, then in Draw picks the bore's rim in the top view for a diameter and finds it called out as `M6×1 × <length> DEEP, Ø5 × 10 DEEP`.

Open: drawing the cosmetic thread itself (the thin major-diameter circle in an end view, its lines in a side view) and hole tables on drawing sheets.

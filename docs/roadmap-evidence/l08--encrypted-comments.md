Slice 4, comments end-to-end encrypted (L11; held for the owner's decisions in #1667). On an encrypted project every member, viewers included, keeps one comment log: a `comments` project envelope (wire code 7, appended to the kinds; 2 MiB plaintext ceiling) holding the comments they wrote and the resolutions they made, encrypted under the project key with a fresh version key, signed by their certified device and chained on the hash of the log it replaces (`/api/e2ee/projects/:id/comments`, D1 `e2ee_project_comment_logs`, migration 0060; sequence only moves forward; erased with its writer, purged with the project's encrypted data on a reverse migration). The account stores ciphertext only: who wrote a log and when stay readable, as for every envelope. The browser opens every member's log (signed by a device of a member of the project in that key epoch, any role; the log's sequence pinned per writer, so the account cannot roll one back; a log signed by someone other than the member it is served as is left out) and merges them (`mergeCommentLogs`): a comment belongs to the log that holds it, a thread is resolved by its author or the owner (latest event wins), and the owner removes others' comments by hiding them. Encrypted share links stay view or tweak only. Evidence: `test/e2ee-comments.test.ts` (real client, Worker and SQLite: a viewer's and the owner's comments, ciphertext only, a viewer's resolution of the owner's thread ignored, owner removal, a log moved to another member left out, a rolled-back log refused), `test/encrypted-comment-logs.test.ts` (parsing and merge rules), `packages/crypto` envelope and body tests (wire code 7, round trip), and the CI smoke case `test/e2e/encrypted-comments.spec.ts` (a comment pinned on an encrypted project, one encrypted log and no plaintext anywhere in the account, read back after a reload).

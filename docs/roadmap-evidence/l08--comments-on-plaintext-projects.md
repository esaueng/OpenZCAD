Slice 2, comments anchored to a point on the model, for members of a plaintext project in the account (plan: docs/plans/l08-l03-review-and-versions-plan.md). File › Review › Comments opens the panel; Add comment, then a press on the model pins a numbered thread at the picked point (model coordinates, surface normal, body and document version), and the press selects nothing. Pins are drawn on the viewport's HUD at their points every frame, hidden behind the camera, and a press on one opens its thread. Threads take replies; the author, an editor or the owner resolves or reopens a thread (resolved ones fold under a count and their pins dim); the author or the owner deletes a comment, a thread with its replies. Viewers comment like any member. Storage is D1 table `project_comments` (migration 0058): 2000 characters per comment, 500 comments per project enforced inside the insert, deleted with the project, writes fenced during account erasure, and an erased account's comments in other people's projects deleted with it. Routes refuse an encrypted project (`assertPlaintextProject`) and answer 503 `COMMENTS_UNAVAILABLE` until the migration is applied; the browser refuses an encrypted project before any request ("Comments are not available on encrypted projects yet"), so nothing about an encrypted project's comments is sent in plaintext; encrypted comments are slice 4. Evidence: `test/project-comments.test.ts` (on the migrated SQLite schema: threads, replies from every role, reply-to-reply and cross-project refusals, resolve and delete permissions, cascade, the 500 bound, the erasure fence, request and anchor parsing), `CommentsPanel.test.tsx`, `projectCommentsApi.test.ts` (no request for an encrypted project), and the CI smoke case `test/e2e/project-comments.spec.ts` (a comment pinned on a saved box with its body and point, a reply, resolve, and the pins gone with the panel).

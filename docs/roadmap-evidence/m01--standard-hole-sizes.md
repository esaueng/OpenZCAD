Slice 2, ISO and ANSI size tables (schema v65, up from v64; no kernel change). A `hole` may store `standard`: `{ system: 'iso' | 'ansi', size, kind: 'clearance' | 'tap-drill', fit? }`. Its table gives the bore's diameter, converted to the document's units, so the stored `diameter` is written as that value but not read. Tables (`packages/shared/src/hole-standards.ts`), each in its standard's own units: ISO 273 clearance holes in the fine, medium and coarse series as close, normal and loose fits, and ISO 2306 tap drills, for every ISO 261 coarse size M1.6–M36; for UNC #0–1 in, the close and free (normal) clearance drills of the common machinist's table (it has no loose fit) and the 75% tap drills. Refused by name: an unknown system or size, a fit on a tap drill, and an ANSI loose fit. Clearing `standard` returns to the stored diameter. It composes with slice 1: every bore on sketch points takes the table's size. A content gate refuses a standard in a document that declares v64 or older.

UI: the Hole tool's Options gain Standard (Any, ISO metric, ANSI inch), Screw, For (Clearance, Tap drill) and Fit. A standard removes the diameter handle, since the table sets it, and the hint reads it out ("M8×1.25 tap drill ⌀ 6.8"); counterbore and countersink defaults follow the table's bore. A History edit reopens with the standard chosen, and Any clears it. History reads "M6 clearance (normal) through".

Evidence: `test/hole-standards.test.ts` (21 cases: every tap drill falls between its thread's basic minor diameter `D − 1.0825 P` and `D − P/2`; every clearance hole clears its screw with each fit wider than the last; the sizes match the M15 ISO 261 table; spot values as printed; labels; refusals; the v65 gate; on the pinned kernel an M6 clearance hole drilled to Ø6.6 whatever the stored diameter, a blind M8 tap drill to Ø6.8 by closed-form volume and back to its stored diameter when cleared, a 1/4-20 tap drill in an inch document to 0.201 in, and two M4 tap drills on sketch points); `apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/tools.test.ts` and `edit.test.ts`; `test/e2e/hole-standards.spec.ts`, which clicks the block's top face, chooses ISO metric, M8 and Tap drill in Options, checks there is no diameter handle, drags the blind depth arrow and checks the stored hole and its History row.

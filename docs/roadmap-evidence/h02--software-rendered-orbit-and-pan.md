Severely GPU-bound orbit and pan can enter the reduced framebuffer on the
first moving draw. Two completed GPU measurements at rest or an observed
backlog qualify the scene. Automatic full-size animation frames wait for
the previous submission. At the 0.75× backing ratio, dense sharp-edge
batches can use one physical pixel per stroke and retain every original
segment and its depth bias. The normal strokes return at rest. Regression
coverage includes a bent edge behind a face, a 33-body cache at capacity,
a zoom crossing the severe threshold, and empty hovers during a slow fade.
The Chromium comparison uses a 1366 × 1024 viewport at 2× density, a
12-second Shift-left-drag for orbit and a 12-second right-drag for pan.
The reported rate counts scene submissions with a changed camera across
the whole gesture, including its onset.

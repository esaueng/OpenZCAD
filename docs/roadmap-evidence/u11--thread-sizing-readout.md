After a cylindrical face pick, Thread shows internal or external, nominal
size and pitch in the command bar during creation and History edits. The
metric size comes from the same rule as the exact build, before clearance
or crest truncation. Pipe threads show their standard designation and
pitch. Chromium covers a shaft, a bore and an NPT choice in
`test/e2e/in-place-thread.spec.ts`.

ADR-024 Phase 8, slice 8-C5b-1: the MLS archive verifier (`packages/mls/crate/src/archive.rs`, `session.rs`) replays from an owner-signed checkpoint as well as from the epoch-0 GroupInfo. A checkpoint is a GroupInfo of epoch K > 0 whose signer is in its tree, is listed as owner by its roles, and is certified by a device that is unrevoked in the current directory, so a stolen owner leaf cannot backdate a checkpoint. Replay seeds removals from the checkpoint and requires exactly records K+1 through the target, and the final removal set must still match the joining GroupInfo exactly. Any other signer is refused as `checkpoint-invalid`. Empty records are now an error rather than a slice underflow in `complete_rejoin`. The committed `packages/mls/pkg` was rebuilt by the reproducible `mls-wasm` workflow on the pinned Linux toolchain (`build.sh --check`); only the WASM and `BUILD.json` changed, not the bindings. Evidence: seven new `checkpoint_tests` in the crate (join from a checkpoint keeps an epoch-2 removal only the checkpoint carries; wrong ranges, a non-owner signer, and a revoked or uncertified owner leaf are refused, with full replay still working; an owner checkpoint that omits removals gets `removal-history-mismatch`; a checkpoint from another chain is refused; rejoin and `complete_rejoin` work from a checkpoint), plus `packages/mls` vitest (142) and `test/e2ee-*.test.ts` (627) against the rebuilt WASM. Producing checkpoints in the app (C5b-2) and replaying them for old-version authorization (C5b-3) follow.

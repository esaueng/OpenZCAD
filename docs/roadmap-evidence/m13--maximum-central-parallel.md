Maximum, central and parallel distances (no schema or kernel change; measurements stay out of the document). Measure gains three modes, each two picks recorded by identity like Distance, with the geometry read from the picks' exact topology at measure and refresh time and solved in closed form (`apps/web/src/lib/measureDistances.ts`). Maximum takes points, straight edges and full circles: over a segment the farthest distance is convex, so it is reached at an end, and from a point to a full circle it is √(h² + (ρ + r)²) at the opposite rim point. Central measures from a circle's centre, or square from a cylinder's or hole's axis, to the other pick. Parallel measures between parallel edges, axes or planar faces and between an edge and a parallel face, and reads two concentric circles or cylinders as their radial difference. Each row draws the span it measured and names itself ("Parallel distance, radial difference"); pairs without a closed form here (an arc's or a circle pair's maximum, a face's maximum, non-parallel pairs, picks with no centre) are refused by name and keep the first pick, never estimated. The kinds `max-distance`, `central-distance` and `parallel-distance` persist and refresh with the other rows. Evidence: `test/entity-distance-measure.test.ts` (13 cases: segment-end, point-to-rim and segment-to-circle maxima; centre and axis central distances; parallel edges, faces, an edge on a face, a radial difference and offset axes; every refusal; a ring wall recorded as its 6 mm radial difference, refreshed to 7 mm when the bore shrinks and kept through the store), `MeasurementDock.test.tsx` and the Chromium smoke case "Maximum records the farthest two edges are apart, and Central says what it needs" in `test/e2e/measure.spec.ts`.

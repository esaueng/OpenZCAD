Curvature and zebra overlays (view state only, no schema change), on the draft overlay's query path and overlay (`surface-analysis` request, `exact-surface-analysis.ts`; one engine with the clash query). Display › Analysis › Curvature maps the kernel's principal curvatures (`getFaceCurvature`, signed convex-positive against the outward normal) at samples on each face's own surface as Gaussian (1/length²), mean or maximum (1/length) curvature, on one scale across the bodies on screen (concave blue, flat grey, convex red; maximum runs grey to red), and names the tightest face radius from `getFaceMinRadius`, labelled exact on analytic faces (closed form) and approximate on NURBS. Face ranges are exact where curvature is constant on the surface type (plane, cylinder, sphere) and sampled on cones, tori and freeform faces; the panel counts the exact ones. Zebra reflects stripes from the kernel's exact outward normals (`evaluateSurfaceNormal`), sampled four times as densely, in a clipped shader that moves with the view; the stripe count only redraws. Limits stated on screen: curvature along edges and where undefined (a cone's apex, a freeform pole) is left grey and listed; the scale spans the largest value found; zebra is read by eye at the sample density and is not a certified G1/G2 check. Evidence: `test/surface-analysis.test.ts` (sphere k = 1/r exact in all three measures with its 5 mm radius in closed form; a bored plate's bore mean −1/12 concave, Gaussian 0, maximum 1/6, radius 6 exact, its planes flat; a torus sampled with Gaussian spanning both signs within ±1/21; zebra unit outward normals, denser than draft's samples), `packages/viewport/src/scene/analysisOverlay.test.ts` (zebra shader, uniforms, clipping), `apps/web/src/lib/analysis/surfaceAnalysisUi.test.ts`, `AnalysisPanel.test.tsx`, `DisplayModeMenu.test.tsx` and the CI smoke case `test/e2e/analysis-curvature-zebra.spec.ts` (a block and an 8 mm ball: mean 0 to 0.125 1/mm with the 8 mm exact radius, Gaussian 0.0156 1/mm², zebra on all 8 faces, cleared on Turn off).

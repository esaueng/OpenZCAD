Trust on first use for invitations (sharing plan slices 1 and 2, owner decisions D1 and D5; [ADR-024 amendment 2026-11-25](docs/adrs/ADR-024-end-to-end-encrypted-projects.md#amendment-2026-11-25-trust-on-first-use-for-invitations)): nobody compares codes. The identity-signed contacts list is the only cross-device trust store: a new `trust` entry (contacts entry version 2, fixed vector) pins someone trusted on first use, not verified. People it pins are added or joined automatically; someone new takes one signed confirmation (one passkey touch or the recovery key) on each side, never in the background (`confirm-new`, `confirm-owner`, `trustPerson`). The opened link is the acceptance (no Accept click). A changed identity blocks until Review or a signed Trust new keys, rechecked at Commit time. The sharing dialog drops the "email only / this contact" choice and shows Verified or Not verified. Statement, email-hash, link-token, acceptance, KeyPackage/leaf and removal-gate checks are unchanged (`packages/crypto/src/contacts.test.ts`, `packages/crypto/src/vectors.test.ts`, `test/e2ee-contacts.test.ts`, `test/e2ee-invitations-browser.test.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/ProjectSharingDialog.test.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/EncryptedInvitations.test.tsx`, `test/e2e/encrypted-sharing.spec.ts`).

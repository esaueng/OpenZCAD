Lock/unlock a whole entity (schema v138). Lock now takes a snap point as before, or a line, circle or arc picked away from its points, and records that entity's resolved geometry in a new `lock-entity` relation; a second Lock on it unlocks it, and locking an entity takes over any point lock already on it in the same undoable edit. On the solver it is exactly as many equations as the entity has freedoms, so it is never redundant with itself: a line's two ends, a circle's centre and radius, an arc's centre and start plus the one end coordinate its own centre–end tie leaves free (chosen by where the end lies, so the five stay independent). Document validation refuses a lock on anything but a line, circle or arc, recorded geometry of the wrong length or with a non-positive radius, a second lock, a whole lock beside a point lock, and a point lock on a locked entity; Trim and Extend drop the lock of the entity they reshape (counted with the other dropped relations) and Fillet refuses a corner on a locked line. Clicking a line at its midpoint now locks the line; the UX-2 midpoint refusal is shown by the point-only tools. Builds on v137 (#1900, on main). Evidence: `test/sketch-constraint-set.test.ts` (on the pinned kernel a locked line, circle and three arcs covering both end-coordinate branches solve at zero DOF with rank equal to equations and one residual row; a radius edit on a locked circle is refused; a locked arc stays put while a joined line moves to it; validation and the v137 refusal), `lib/sketch/constraints.test.ts`, `filletBatch.test.ts` and `test/e2e/sketch-constraint-set.spec.ts` ("Lock holds a whole line, replacing its point lock, and unlocks it", undone step by step).

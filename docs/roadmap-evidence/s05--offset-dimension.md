A driving offset dimension (schema v146). Offset now writes one `offset` relation alongside its copies: every source/copy pair with its side (a line's left or right of its stored direction, a round's outside or inside) and one driving value, the distance as typed (an expression stays parametric). On the solver a line copy keeps that signed distance from its source line at both ends, and a round copy keeps its radius that much from the source's, measured along a ray from the source centre through two helper points that add exactly as many equations as parameters; direction and concentricity stay with the optional links Offset keeps only where a solve says they add rank, so the relation never makes an offset redundant. The relation is listed with the sketch's constraints ("Offset 1 · Line 5 from Line 1 +3") and its value edits like any driving dimension, re-offsetting every copy in one undoable edit, round joins included; a non-positive value is refused by name (the side stays with each copy). Deleting or trimming a copy or its source drops only that pair. Builds on v145 (the S05 pattern relation, #1917). Evidence: `test/sketch-modify-tools.test.ts` (a v145 document holding an offset dimension is refused; on the pinned kernel: mitered and round offsets of a rectangle still solve with rank equal to equations, and re-driven from 1 to 2.5 the copies sit 2.5 out with radius-2.5 joins; an inward circle offset re-driven to 2 has radius 3, a lone arc's to 1.5 keeps its centre), `edits.test.ts`, `constraints.test.ts` and `test/e2e/sketch-modify-tools.spec.ts` ("the Offset distance stays a driving dimension that re-offsets every copy").

A driving offset dimension (schema v146). Offset now writes one `offset` relation alongside its copies: every source/copy pair with its side (a line's left or right of its stored direction, a round's outside or inside) and one driving value, the distance as typed (an expression stays parametric). On the solver a line copy keeps that signed distance from its source line at both ends, and a round copy keeps its radius that much from the source's, measured along a ray from the source centre through two helper points that add exactly as many equations as parameters; direction and concentricity stay with the optional links Offset keeps only where a solve says they add rank, so the relation never makes an offset redundant. The relation is listed with the sketch's constraints ("Offset 1 · Line 5 from Line 1 +3") and its value edits like any driving dimension, re-offsetting every copy in one undoable edit, round joins included; a non-positive value is refused by name (the side stays with each copy). Deleting or trimming a copy or its source drops only that pair. Every free copy is started where the relation puts it (a round concentric with its source at the offset radius, a line square onto its offset carrier), so dragging a source carries its copies and a new value moves only the copies. The equations leave a copy's side free, so every solve reads each copy back (a line copy's signed distance at both ends, a round copy's reach past the source radius) and a copy on the wrong side fails the relation; a chain's corner shared by two collinear sources is stated once; a full-turn arc offsets to a circle and pairs with it; renaming a parameter rewrites the offset value and a parameter edit re-solves it; a value whose expression is not positive is refused. Builds on v145 (the S05 pattern relation, #1917). Evidence: `test/sketch-modify-tools.test.ts` (a v145 document holding an offset dimension is refused; a wrong-side round copy is refused; a collinear chain stays non-redundant; a full-turn arc offsets to a circle; a renamed parameter follows and `1 - 3` is refused; on the pinned kernel: mitered and round offsets of a rectangle still solve with rank equal to equations, and re-driven from 1 to 2.5 the copies sit 2.5 out with radius-2.5 joins; an inward circle offset re-driven to 2 has radius 3, a lone arc's to 1.5 keeps its centre), `edits.test.ts`, `constraints.test.ts` and `test/e2e/sketch-modify-tools.spec.ts` ("the Offset distance stays a driving dimension that re-offsets every copy" and the pick-and-drag "dragging an offset source circle carries its copy at the offset distance").

Arc and circle inputs to Fillet and Chamfer (schema v141). The Fillet tool's corner markers now also mark where a line meets an arc or two arcs meet end to end (a smooth continuation offers none), and where a free line or arc end lies on a circle — two markers there, one each way round. The fillet is the exact circle of the asked radius tangent to both legs: its centre is the intersection of the legs' offsets (parallel lines at ±r, concentric circles at R ± r) whose two tangent points lie on the legs themselves, nearest the joint. Arc legs are cut back to their tangencies in the same one-transaction batch as lines, with their preview; a circle stays whole, and what held the cut end on it is released. Picking both sides of one joint is refused by name. The fillet records its tangency to each leg: a line as before, an arc or a circle through a new curve-to-curve `tangent` form (two arcs joined end to end touch where the shared point is in line with both centres; an arc end on a circle is on the circle and in line with both centres), so the next solve keeps it tangent. Chamfer takes a line or an arc against an arc, cut back by the same straight setback (a chord on an arc), or against a circle on the side that was clicked, the bevel held on the circle. Builds on v137 (#1900, on main); v138–v140 are claimed by the S07 entity lock (#1913) and the S05 pattern relation and offset dimension. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/filletCurved.test.ts` (line–arc and arc–arc fillets against closed-form tangency, no corner on a smooth joint, a line into a circle either side; each committed candidate solves on the pinned kernel with rank equal to equations and nothing moving), `curveChamfer.test.ts` (line–arc, arc–arc either pick order, a circle on the clicked side solved on the pinned kernel, refusals), `test/sketch-curved-tangent.test.ts` (inside and outside arc tangency and an arc on a circle against closed form, the join requirement, the v140 refusal) and `test/e2e/sketch-modify-tools.spec.ts` ("Fillet and Chamfer meet a circle a line ends on, keeping the circle whole": the two corner markers picked on the canvas, radius and setback entered, undo between).

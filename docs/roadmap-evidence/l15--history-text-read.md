Slice A, history text read path ([design](docs/plans/l15-scripting-design.md)): a new lazily loaded `@openzcad/scripting` package prints the document's history (`printHistory`: `openzcad history 1`, typed parameters, every feature kind in history order, sketches with their objects, node metadata) and reads it back against the document (`readHistoryText`) with line-and-column diagnostics. Expressions sit in backticks only where document-core's own expression walker says a field is one, and are evaluated with the field's F05 quantity (an expression the document already cannot evaluate is a warning, a new one an error); ids print as `#id` and must resolve; stored V5 picks print as `face(#feature, "lineage")` / `edge(…)` with their implied hashes left out and are restored from the stored witness, never invented; another version, other units, an unknown kind, field or reference and a parameter cycle are refused by name. The History panel's header gains History as text, a read-only dialog loaded on demand that follows every edit. No schema change. Regression evidence: `packages/scripting/src/history-text.test.ts` (print shape, determinism, round trip of awkward values, comments and layout, detached sketches, typed refusals), `test/history-text-corpus.test.ts` (the identity law over 19 fixture documents (kernel-built with real V5 references, parameter-driven and legacy): the text reads back to exactly the history the document holds), `HistoryTextDialog.test.tsx` and `ModelPanel.test.tsx`.

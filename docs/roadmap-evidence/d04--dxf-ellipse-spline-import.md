DXF ellipse and spline import and the sketch menu export ([design](docs/plans/sketch-dxf-export-plan.md#dxf-ellipse-and-spline-import-d04)): ELLIPSE imports as the sketch's exact ellipse or elliptical arc (a -Z ellipse reversed to run counter-clockwise from +Z), and SPLINE as its exact control spline wherever the sketch object is the same curve: clamped uniform degree 2 or 3 (knots matched up to an affine change of parameter, within 1e-9 of their range), periodic uniform ones as closed splines, equal weights as non-rational, degree 1 as lines, and a clamped loop split by knot insertion into two halves that close a region. Unequal weights, other knot vectors, higher degrees, over 64 control points and fit-point-only splines are counted by name; malformed lists refuse the file. A sketch's history menu gains Export DXF…, opening the export on that sketch as DXF. Evidence: `test/dxf-import-curves.test.ts` (every imported ellipse and spline compared point for point with the file's own curve evaluated from its definition, -Z ellipses, full-turn ranges, units, regions closed by a diameter, a periodic ring and three split loops, each refusal and malformed list by name, the undoable sketch), `test/dxf-import.test.ts` (refusal counts and status) and `test/e2e/dxf-curve-import.spec.ts` (an inch cam of a periodic SPLINE around an ELLIPSE bore imported and extruded; a sketch exported from its menu as DXF). With this the row's open list is closed: the sketch menu entry and ellipse/spline import here (a single-span SPLINE is a Bézier, imported exactly), flattened views with the drawing DXF export (#1640), and the unit choice for unitless files with I04 (#752, `ImportUnitsDialog`; `test/dxf-import.test.ts` "says how a unitless file was read"). A sketch holding an ellipse or spline still exports as SVG, its DXF refused by name, since R12 has neither entity.

Body rename and folders slice (research audit ZC-127/128, schema v34): a live body renames in place in Objects (its row's rename button, F2 or a double-click; Enter keeps, Escape cancels, empty or unchanged is no edit) as one undoable `node.rename`; features, booleans and drawing views name bodies by id, so nothing rebinds. Objects folders are document content (`ProjectDocument.objectFolders`, additive; absent means none): New folder takes the selected bodies, a folder renames like a body, nests, collapses (a per-device preference), selects and hides or shows everything inside it, and deletes without losing what it held. Bodies and folders drag in and out (a Move-to-top-level target appears while dragging), and a folder cannot be dropped inside itself. A body without an explicit placement follows the body it was made from, so a fillet or boolean result keeps its folder. Every folder edit is one undoable `objectFolder.*` command kept out of the geometry rebuild key; a search lists matches flat and clearing it brings the tree back. Evidence: `test/object-folders.test.ts` (schema pin and additivity, create/rename/nest/move/delete with undo/redo/replay, lineage through a boolean, geometry key unchanged, malformed sections refused on load and at the account edge, folder-only undo decided from recorded changes, names kept out of diagnostics), `test/body-rename.test.ts` (a union on a renamed body survives an edit of its feature, a reload and a replay with the same volume), `ObjectsPanel.test.tsx` / `ObjectsPanel.folders.test.tsx` and `objects-folders.spec.ts` (rename with undo/redo, folder from a selection, drag in and out, collapse and hide, all surviving a reload). Open: a keyboard way to move a body between folders, and I02's STEP hierarchy landing as nested folders.

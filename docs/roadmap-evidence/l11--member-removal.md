Phase 5, slice 5-5, member removal and leaving in end-to-end encrypted projects, behind the same flags as slices 5-2 to 5-4 (`E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED`, `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED`, the sharing rollout), with migration 0039 `e2ee_mls_proposals`. The owner removes a member by an MLS Commit whose `dropUsers` takes the identity out of the roles with every leaf it holds (`removeMember` in `projectGroups.ts`; the sharing dialog's encrypted branch offers Remove, with the copy that removal keeps someone out of every later version and not what they already opened); the Worker deletes the member's `project_members` row with a `member-removed` event in the Commit's own batch (slice 5-4's stated-change check), and, gated on that event having landed, its account-wrapped keys of the project (housekeeping, not revocation) and its assistant consent rows (closing 6-1's gap). A member leaves by a SelfRemove proposal, never a Commit of its own (RFC 9420 §12.2): its device first removes the account's other leaves in a Commit of its own, then posts the proposal (`leaveProject`; Leave project… in the dialog, confirmed after saying access goes at once), which `POST /api/e2ee/projects/:id/mls/proposals` stores after checking its framing (a member's PublicMessage proposal of the SelfRemove type for this group and epoch, `parseSelfRemoveProposal`) and, in the same batch, drops the member's access (decision M4): the row, the wraps and the consent go then, not when someone commits. Every member Commit for an epoch must settle every proposal still pending in it, as applied by reference or as rejected by the committer's engine (`proposals`, `rejectedProposals`), or the Worker refuses it (409 `E2EE_PROPOSALS_PENDING`; the Commit row's insert is gated on exactly that set), so a Commit that raced a departure cannot leave it behind and a bad proposal cannot hold the group; an external Commit (a rejoin, which cannot carry proposals) is refused while one is pending (409 `E2EE_DEPARTURE_PENDING`). In the browser every Commit first fetches and processes the proposals of its epoch (only a SelfRemove of the sender's own leaf, for this group and epoch, reaches the engine, since any other cached proposal would make every Commit of the epoch break the policy), receivers process the proposals a Commit applied before it (`processCommit`), and writers commit pending departures before their next write (`ensureProjectRotated`, from the group record's `pendingProposals`); any member may commit one, a viewer included, and the page does whenever it reads the member's role (`commitPendingDepartures`). A departure leaves the identity listed in the roles without a leaf, which only the owner changes: the owner's next write drops identities that hold no leaf and that the server reports as departed, so no other device of a leaver can rejoin under the MLS rejoin rules. A Commit that applies a departure or drops someone replaces the dedupe secret (decision M5), and a pending Commit is discarded on crash recovery while proposals are pending. `ProjectGroupContext.onMembersRemoved` (and `subscribeMemberRemovals` in `encryptedProjects.ts`) is told whenever a Commit this page sent or processed took other members' last leaf: the hook for slice 5-10 to offer reissuing the project's share links. A removed or departed member's page reads no role (the Worker answers 404) and is held in View with "Waiting for project access"; a member who left goes back to the projects. Migration 0039 has a cached probe that answers "missing" on any failure (`isE2eeMlsProposalStorageReady`): without it the proposal listing is empty and says so, leaving answers 503, and Commits, the owner's removals, role changes and writes work as before; project purge deletes the table's rows and account erasure (`all`) clears their sender, as for Commits. Plaintext sharing, removal and roles are unchanged. Evidence: `test/e2ee-member-removal.test.ts` (Node devices of three accounts, the in-process MLS engine and the real Worker over SQLite: an owner's removal dropping the identity, its row, wraps and consent in the Commit, replacing the dedupe secret and telling the hook, after which the removed member reads no role, holds no key of the new epoch even when a lying server serves it the Commits, cannot rejoin to write and is refused by the Worker; a pending departure of the removed member committed before the drop; removals by non-owners refused by the browser and the Worker; a member leaving from one of its two devices, its other leaf removed first, its access gone at the proposal, the departure committed by a viewer reading its role with a new dedupe secret, the hook told on the viewer's and the owner's devices, and the identity dropped by the owner's next write; an editor committing a departure before its write; the owner's leaving, a non-SelfRemove message, a Commit that leaves a departure out and a rejoin while one is pending refused, and a proposal with a forged signature rejected while the real one is applied; without migration 0039, removals, role changes, writes and purge working and leaving answering 503; erasure and purge of stored proposals), `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/mls/epochRoles.test.ts` (the SelfRemove framing checked against the engine), `ProjectSharingDialog.test.tsx`, `d1-migration-split.test.ts`, and Playwright `encrypted-member-removal.spec.ts` (Alice removes Bob from the sharing dialog: his row and wraps go, the Worker answers his page 404 and his page holds him in View with "Waiting for project access"; Bob, a viewer, leaves from the dialog: his access goes when the proposal is stored, his page returns to the projects, and Alice's next edit commits the departure and drops him from the roles). Device-list lookups and the contacts list no longer outlive what they were for (#898 and #902 review): an accepted invitation relates its two sides only while it is open (an encrypted one until its Add completes it or it expires), so a removed member loses the relation and the email with the membership, while the owner and current members of a project still read the device list of anyone who held a leaf in it, so a removed member's versions keep verifying (`test/e2ee-contacts.test.ts`, `test/e2ee-member-lists.test.ts`).

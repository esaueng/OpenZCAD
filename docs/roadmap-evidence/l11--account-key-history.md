Phase 3 tail, slice 3T-1 (account-key history and revocation replacement), behind the off-by-default `E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED` flag: every account-key rotation (device or passkey removal, read-only revocation, recovery-key replacement) now wraps the replaced account key under its successor with the `previous-account-key` AES-KW purpose and stores it in the new `e2ee_account_key_history` table (migration `0030_e2ee_account_key_history.sql`, not applied anywhere remote; rows reference the user, survive identity pruning, are erased with the other key tables and fenced during erasure). Keys are named by identity generation and the device-list sequence of the entry that first committed to them (`accountKeySequence`). The Worker refuses a rotation or identity replacement that omits or misnames its history wrap, refuses one on a setup or a non-rotating append, stores it in the same batch, and serves only the caller's own rows (`GET /api/e2ee/account-key-history`); routes stay closed and erasure keeps working while 0030 is missing. `openAccountKeyChain` walks back from the unlocked key to any older one, checking current-identity keys against their certified commitments. A device revocation can name its replacement (`Revocation.replacedBy`, signed device-list entry version 2, used only when a replacement is named, so version 1 entries and vectors are unchanged byte for byte); `removeDevice` names the removing certified device. `replaceRecoveryKey` now holds the same device lock as the other rotations. The per-project revocation cutoff statement (`"OZR"`, identity-signed (projectId, cutoffEpoch) pairs bound to user, generation and revoking entry) is defined with encode, sign and verify only; storage and enforcement follow in Phase 4. Evidence: `test/e2ee-account-keys.test.ts` (three rotations open all four keys, across five replaced and pruned identities; a removed device's key opens none of the newer ones; tampered or missing rows refused; Worker refusals; only the caller's history served; erasure and cascade; the erasure fence; the pre-0030 state; the replacement lock serializing a removal and a second replacement; a history-write case in the mutation-authority matrix), `packages/crypto/src/devices.test.ts`, the version 2 entry and cutoff vectors in `packages/crypto/src/vectors.test.ts`, the backward-only chain in `wrap.test.ts`, the new cases in `fuzz.test.ts`, and `test/d1-migration-split.test.ts`.

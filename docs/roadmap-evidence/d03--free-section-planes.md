Free section planes (schema v91, built on v90; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#free-section-planes-d03-schema-v91)): a section line drawn at an angle across a front, top or side view (more than 10° off horizontal or vertical; through the press point, snapped to the view's middle or a circle's centre) cuts the part by an inclined plane, and a click beside it places the section seen square to that plane from that side, placed freely with its `SECTION A-A` caption. `DrawingSectionCut.frame` stores the frame like an auxiliary view's; the geometry worker's exact half-space boolean turns its box into the plane's frame, so the cut outline is kernel-sampled exact edges and the cut face is hatched, with the tessellation fallback unchanged; principal sections cut exactly as before. A content gate refuses a frame below v91. Evidence: `test/drawing-free-sections.test.ts` (frame validation by name, the projection frame and plane, the v83 gate, a free section staying put when its source moves and its plane moving along its normal, the tool's free and square lines and its placement and frame, and on a kernel block a 45° cut through its middle: one exact cut face, Z√2 edges, a hatched area of Z√2 × Y, the line at 45° through the source's middle with arrows along the sight, letters and caption) and `test/e2e/drawing-free-sections.spec.ts` (a 45° drag across a block's front view, the section placed beside it, hatched, exact, exported to SVG and reopened). Still open: stepped section lines and lineage for cut edges.

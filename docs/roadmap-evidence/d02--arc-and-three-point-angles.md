Arc and three-point angles (schema v82, built on v81; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#arc-and-three-point-angles-d02-schema-v82)): the Arc angle tool (E) dimensions a face-on arc's included angle at its centre, the way the arc runs (past 180° when it does, its dimension arc sweeping the long way), and the 3-point angle tool (J) the angle at a vertex point between two others (edge ends, circle centres or marks), completing the row's dimension set with the two-edge angle. Both are read from the model on every draw, take tolerances, affixes and the sheet's angle format, lay out and export through the angle's page model, and break by name (`closed-edge`, `edge-on`, `foreshortened`, `no-angle`, `mark-missing`) rather than rebinding. A content gate refuses them below v82. Evidence: `test/drawing-angle-dimensions.test.ts` (validation by name and a mark on another view refused; on kernel bodies an R3 fillet's quarter arc reading 90° with rays of R3, a toleranced `90° ±0.5°`, a whole circle refused; the long-way sweep of an arc past 180°; the angle at a pin's centre subtended by a box edge in the same plane, the same from the pin's top refused as foreshortened and a vertex on its own arm as `no-angle`; drafts from both tools, a mark as the vertex, a re-pick keeping its format and the v82 gate) and `test/e2e/drawing-angles.spec.ts` (an imported block rounded R5: its quarter arc and three corners each read 90°, export to SVG and reopen).

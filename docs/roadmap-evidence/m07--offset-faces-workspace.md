Multi-face Offset, workspace slice: two or more faces picked with Shift or a
Face-filter box sweep offer Offset on the selection rail; it arms the one
single-face offset handle and keypad on the last-picked face and commits one
`offset-faces` step, "Offset 2 faces · +2 mm" in History, whose distance is
changed again from its reopened History card. A set that is not one plane is
refused by name before a handle appears; kernel refusals reach the card as on
the single-face path. `apps/web/src/lib/faceSetOffset.test.ts`,
`test/offset-faces-workspace.test.ts`, `test/e2e/offset-face-set.spec.ts`.

Align, face to face (slice 5b, owner decision D3): a new in-viewport Align tool picks a flat face on the body to move, then a flat face on another body, and Done stores one ordinary Move (rotation, pivot and translation as numbers; no new feature kind, no schema change, no link to the target, so editing the target later does not move the aligned body). The turn is the smallest one taking the moving face's outward normal against the target's (none when already opposed; half a turn about `unit(n × X)`, or `unit(n × Y)` when |n·X| ≥ 0.9, when they match; parallel within a sine of 1e-9), about the moving face's centroid; the translation runs along the target normal only, plus a signed Offset gap, with Flip for faces pointing the same way. Move's stored ZYX angles are now extracted with `atan2` (well conditioned at gimbal lock), so the replayed Move keeps a 10 m face flat to the 1e-6 mm tolerance for normals near a quarter turn about each axis and for random normals (`test/m10-face-align-precision.test.ts`); a click on the moved preview maps back to the committed face it came from. Curved faces, a target on the moving body and a body a later feature used up are refused in plain sentences. Covered by the transform math (`apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/faceAlign.test.ts`), the two-face pick in the command (`apps/web/src/hooks/useInPlaceCommandAlign.test.tsx`), exact-kernel boxes for bounds, contact plane, unchanged volume, Undo, Redo, reload and replay (`test/m10-face-align.test.ts`), and a browser pick, pick, preview, Done and Undo (`test/e2e/face-align.spec.ts`).

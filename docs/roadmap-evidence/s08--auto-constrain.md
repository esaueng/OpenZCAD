First slice: inference engine and Auto-constrain setting. A drawing commit (line, arc, circle, ellipse, spline, rectangle/polygon composite) adds the relations its placement satisfies exactly, in the same undo step as the entity. Always kept: an end landing on another curve's end is joined (coincident) and an end on a line's midpoint is held there (midpoint). With the new `sketching.autoConstrain` setting on (Settings and the sketch palette; off by default, absent in older settings reads off): horizontal/vertical lines, a line perpendicular to the oblique line it continues, a line leaving an arc end along its tangent and an arc leaving a line end tangentially (`tangent` with the arc's contact point), an end or centre on a centre (coincident), an end snapped to the origin (locked at 0,0) and an end snapped to a line crossing (held on both lines). A hold on a curve or the origin is proposed only from the snap the pointer engaged, never from a grid point that merely lines up. While drawing, Automatic inferencing now also locks a line to the perpendicular of a line ending at its anchor and to the tangent of an arc ending there, and slides an arc's centre onto a line's normal when its start lands on that line's end; an exact snap wins over every lock. Optional relations are kept only where a solve on the pinned kernel says they add rank (`settleOptionalRelations`), so inference never makes a sketch redundant and never moves geometry. No silent constraints: the status line names every inferred relation, the last commit's inferred glyphs are drawn even when glyphs show only the selection's, and each is an ordinary relation that a glyph click and Delete (or Undo) removes. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/inference.test.ts` (rules, locks, refusals of unhinted holds, composite corner deduplication), `test/sketch-inference.test.ts` (pinned-kernel solves: a hand-drawn rectangle is joined and squared at 2 DOF, a rotated rectangle drawn by perpendicular locks keeps one closing perpendicular at 5 DOF, a restated horizontal is dropped, arc-tangent lines stay put, setting-off keeps only joins and midpoints, undo and delete), `relationGlyphs.test.ts`, `test/app-settings.test.ts`, and `test/e2e/sketch-inference.spec.ts` (a chain drawn by clicks keeps coincident, horizontal and vertical relations, a glyph click and Delete removes one, Undo restores it and then the closing line with both relations, setting off keeps only a midpoint).

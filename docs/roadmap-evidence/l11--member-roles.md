Phase 5, slice 5-4, role changes in end-to-end encrypted projects, behind the same flags as slices 5-2 to 5-3b (`E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED`, `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED`, the sharing rollout), with no migration. The owner changes a member's role by an MLS Commit whose `setRoles` every member's policy accepts only from the owner (`changeMemberRole` in `projectGroups.ts`; the sharing dialog's encrypted branch now offers a role control per member, through `EncryptedSharingClient.changeRole`). A Commit states the membership change it makes (`E2eeMlsCommitRequest.roles` and `dropUsers`), and the Worker (`apps/web/worker/e2eeMembership.ts`) checks the statement and mirrors it into `project_members` in the Commit's own batch: only the owner states a change, only for current members, the mirror and a `member-role-changed` (or `member-removed`) access event land with the Commit or not at all, and the Commit row's insert is gated on every target still holding the role the check read. Every Commit carries its new epoch's GroupInfo; the Worker reads the roles extension from it (`parseGroupInfoContext` in `mls/epochRoles.ts`) and from the stored GroupInfo of the current epoch, and refuses (409 `E2EE_ROLES_MISMATCH`) any Commit whose new roles are not the current ones with exactly the stated changes applied (an Add's invitee with its invitation's role, the stated role changes and drops), so an unstated role change, or a stated one the Commit does not make, never reaches the mirror. Ownership never moves on an encrypted project: the browser refuses to build it and the Worker refuses an `owner` role, a change that targets the owner, and any new GroupInfo naming another owner (409 `E2EE_OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER_UNAVAILABLE`), though the MLS policy alone would allow a hand-over. A pending Commit that changes roles is discarded on crash recovery rather than resent without its statement. In the page, a member's role in someone else's encrypted project comes from the project's MLS roles (`memberships.ts` `ownProjectRole`: this device's session after catching up, or the epoch's key archive on a device without a leaf), never the server's mirror, read on open, on focus, every 20 s and when a write is refused as read-only (`useEncryptedMemberRole`): an editor edits and autosaves through the encrypted store (no room or lease), a viewer gets the existing read-only UI (View mode, no Build tools, edits refused with "This shared project is read-only"). The edit-lease rule that 5-3a carried over from the plaintext invitation route no longer applies to encrypted projects: it keeps a plaintext editor's writes in the lease-checked room instead of the account route, while an encrypted project's editors write versions the account accepts only on top of the head they read, and its room serves only the owner until slice 5-6 (which decides the room's leases); the canary recipient rule still applies, and editor invitations and role changes are offered in the encrypted dialog whatever the lease rollout. Plaintext sharing, roles and leases are unchanged. Evidence: `test/e2ee-member-roles.test.ts` (Node devices of three accounts, the in-process MLS engine and the real Worker over SQLite: an editor demoted, the mirror and event moved with the Commit, its earlier version verified by a fresh owner browser, its next write refused by its own browser before any request and, forced with leaked keys, by the Worker, and refused by every browser when a lying server keeps the editor row; a non-owner's role change refused by its browser, its MLS policy and the Worker; the owner's change sent unstated, and a stated change the Commit does not make, refused with nothing changed; ownership transfer refused by the browser and, stated or not, by the Worker; the account's role read from the MLS roles even when the mirror lies, also on a device without a leaf; a crash-pending role change discarded, not resent), `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/mls/epochRoles.test.ts` (GroupInfo roles parsed and checked against the engine), `ProjectSharingDialog.test.tsx`, the updated `test/e2ee-invitations.test.ts`, and Playwright `encrypted-roles.spec.ts` (Alice shares with Bob as an editor through slice 5-3b's flow; Bob models and his version lands; Alice demotes him from the sharing dialog; Bob's next edit is refused by his browser, which switches to View with the Build tools gone; a write forced from his page gets 403 `E2EE_PROJECT_READ_ONLY` from the Worker; Alice reopens and reads his earlier version).

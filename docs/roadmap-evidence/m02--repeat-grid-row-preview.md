Changing a grid’s second-axis count can reuse a byte-identical first row. A 10×3 to 10×10 edit on the 50-copy bar took 1572 to 918 ms median in three matched Chromium pairs. The [benchmark record](docs/performance/2026-11-20-grid-preview-row-reuse.md) includes the initial preview, count edits, and corrected historical timings.

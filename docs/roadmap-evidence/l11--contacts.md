Phase 5, slice 5-1, contacts and verified pins, behind `E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED`, with migration 0037 `e2ee_contact_entries`. `packages/crypto/src/contacts.ts` adds the identity-signed, hash-chained contacts list ("OZK" v1, documented in the package README): each entry verifies a contact's identity key with the device-list head it was compared at, moves that head forward, records a key change or withdraws trust; only a verified contact can advance, change or be untrusted, and only under the identity it was verified with. `verifyContactsList` checks the chain under the identity key and, with a pin, refuses a rolled-back or forked list; `contactDeviceListPin` gives the pin a verified contact's device list must extend. The Worker (`apps/web/worker/e2eeContacts.ts`) serves `GET`/`POST /api/e2ee/contacts` (the caller's list for its current identity generation and the people it may look up; an append must verify under the identity and the list's rules and extend the stored head, checked again with the generation and the session/erasure predicate in the insert) and `GET /api/e2ee/users/:id/device-list`, answered only for co-members of a project or the two sides of a live invitation (decision M2), with one identical 404 for everyone else, a missing id and a malformed one. A cached schema probe keeps ordinary traffic, the other key routes, device-list lookups and erasure working without 0037 (only the contacts list answers 503, and the migration is noticed without a redeploy); erasure deletes the list with the profile, and a replaced identity starts a fresh list. In the browser, `contacts.ts` and `contactPins.ts` keep seen pins and the own list pin in their own IndexedDB database (`openzcad-e2ee-contacts`); a contact's device list must extend both the signed verified head and the newest head this browser accepted, a verified contact whose identity changed drops back to seen with the downgrade kept locally until the key change is signed, signing asks for a passkey or the recovery key (`withIdentityKey`), and only a verified contact yields a key to wrap to.

Selection card (2026-10-24): a direct pick no longer draws a chip on the model or opens the details card. The pick's name, measurement and verbs (Offset/Resize/Sketch/Hole, Fillet/Chamfer/All N edges, Move/Mirror, Union) move into a Selection card above the left verb rail. It hangs off the column float so the rail never moves, pairs its verbs into two columns on stages under 860 px tall, and stands beside the rail's top under 640 px. An armed operation's refusal and its ways out (Edit culprit, Keep last valid, View details) join the bottom notice lane over the status row, and the Undo toast stacks above it via `--lane-refusal`. The details card opens only for a History row or a running tool, never for a pick (viewport or Objects row), so a body's volume and mass read from its History row; modeling-operation cards (hole, shell, sweep, loft, mirror, draft…) now carry the same body readout when they edit an existing feature. An imported STEP face with no armed operation keeps its own card through the Selection card: Edit face for a hole or blend (resize/remove, disabled while editing is locked), Face details for any other recognized or declined face. The viewer keeps the plain name label wherever there is no card (View, Tweak, a running tool) and keeps the numbered labels on several picked bodies. Evidence: `SelectionCard.test.tsx` (verbs, clear, phase marking, validating hold, select-all edges, refusal kept out of the card, refusal row and its three ways out), `Inspector.test.tsx` "direct pick details" (no card for inferred face, edge, body or unclaimed face until asked; a History-pinned feature still opens), `inspector-object-panel.spec.ts` (Objects-row and viewport picks open no card; History opens and deletes the feature), and the `bodyCardFromHistory` e2e fixture every volume read now uses.

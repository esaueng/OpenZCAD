Phase 3 tail, the MLS client in the app (slice 3T-2): `apps/web` depends on `@openzcad/mls` and drives it from `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/mls/`, reachable only from code behind the off-by-default `E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED` availability (nothing calls it yet). `loader.ts` imports the package and starts its module Worker on first use, one per page; the wrapper, Worker and WASM stay out of the launcher (`LAZY_ENTRY_PATTERNS`, an `openzcad-mls` launcher marker and an approved 1.5 MiB lazy-asset budget for the 1,047,287-byte WASM). `sessionStore.ts` keeps a separate IndexedDB database, `openzcad-e2ee-mls` (the device-key database is untouched), with a non-extractable AES-256-GCM state key per account and, per account and project, the sealed session, the certified leaf and the bytes of any Commit the room has not ruled on; every operation runs under a per-project Web Lock and writes with compare-and-swap on a local revision, so a stale tab is refused and re-reads instead of forking. `certify.ts` certifies the leaf key with the stored device signing key through `ecdsaSign` (WebCrypto or Keychain-held keys), as `u8 1 | r || s` over the package's leaf certification payload. `directory.ts` builds the policy's `DeviceDirectory` from verified device lists plus leaf certifications, verifying each signature against the certifying device's key and refusing forged, unattributable or duplicate records; a revoked device needs a per-project revocation cutoff the device list cannot supply yet, so it is refused explicitly (`revocation-cutoff-missing`) rather than defaulted, and no `certifiedEpoch` or `replacedBy` is set (recovery Adds and the last-leaf exception stay unavailable until later slices bind them). Key-session teardown frees every session, forgets the state keys and stops the Worker; operations in flight fail without writing. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/mls/projectSessions.test.ts` (create, seal and reopen to the same project key; cross-tab re-read; a lock-less race refused by compare-and-swap with the loser re-reading; pending Commits confirmed or discarded across a reload; teardown), `directory.test.ts` (forged, wrong-project and wrong-device certifications, unknown devices and users, revoked devices with and without cutoffs, and the policy refusing uncertified and revoked leaves), `sessionStore.test.ts` (both backends), `test/bundle-size-policy.test.ts`, and `test/e2e/mls-client.spec.ts` (the built app's Worker under the shipped CSP creates a group, reloads and reopens the same key in Chromium, and in WebKit where installed).

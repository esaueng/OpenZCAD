Device removal (slices 4-10 and 5-7) now stores the revocation and its identity-signed cutoff statement in one write, with no migration and behind the same flags (`E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED`, `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED`, migration 0036). Before, revoking a device (`POST /api/e2ee/device-list`) and storing its statement (`PUT /api/e2ee/revocation-cutoffs`) were two requests, and a browser that went offline in between left a revoked device without a statement, which co-members could not tell from a device revoked before the account joined their project, so they kept writing under a key the removed device may still derive. The removal participant (`deviceRemoval.ts`) now signs the statement for the entry about to be appended (`cutoffsFor`, sharing its signing with `written`) and the append carries it (`E2eeAppendDeviceListRequest.cutoffs`); `accountKeys.ts` sends it and, on `E2EE_CUTOFFS_INCOMPLETE`, signs once more and retries, and Settings' wrappers (`pageDeviceRemoval.ts`, `projectRemovalStep.ts`) forward it. The Worker (`appendedRevocationCutoffs` in `e2eeDeviceRemoval.ts`, called by `handleAppend`) requires it when the entry revokes a device of an account with encrypted projects owned or shared (recycle bin included): missing is refused with 409 `E2EE_CUTOFFS_REQUIRED`; it must verify against the list including the entry, name that entry, stay within the next key epoch (400 otherwise) and name every such project at its next key epoch (409 `E2EE_CUTOFFS_INCOMPLETE` otherwise); it is inserted in the entry's own D1 batch under the same guard. `written` and `complete` still sign and store the statement again (raising cutoffs after a late rotation); passkey and read-only rotations, accounts without encrypted projects, accounts with encrypted projects switched off and databases before migration 0036 are unchanged, and the device-list body limit grows by one statement's size. Evidence: `test/e2ee-device-removal.test.ts` (a revocation without cutoffs, from a participant or none, is refused and leaves entries, wraps, history and cutoffs unchanged; statements missing a project, an epoch behind or ahead, naming an earlier revoking entry or with another signature are refused with nothing written; a removal cut off the moment its entry lands leaves the statement already stored, so the account's other device reads the removed phone's earlier versions and rotates before its next write; a project created between the listing and the append is refused once and signed again; passkey removal and an account without encrypted projects are unchanged), `test/e2ee-shared-device-removal.test.ts` (a member's removal cut off right after the append: the owner reads the statement, keeps reading the phone's earlier version, and is refused `E2EE_MEMBER_ROTATION_PENDING` by its browser and the Worker's fence until another of the member's devices rotates) and `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/projectRemovalStep.test.ts`.

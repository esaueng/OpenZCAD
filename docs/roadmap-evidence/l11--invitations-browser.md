Phase 5, slice 5-3b, the browser flow for invitations to encrypted projects, behind the same flags as 5-3a (`E2EE_KEYS_ENABLED`, `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED`, the sharing rollout) and migration 0038, with no migration of its own. `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/invitations.ts`: the owner's certified device signs the "OZI" statement and keeps the invitee's address in this browser (`invitationStore.ts`; the server keeps its hash); the invitee accepts the `#encrypted-invite=` link, checks the statement against the owner's device list and, once the owner is a verified contact (5-1's mutual comparison), each of its certified devices publishes one KeyPackage whose leaf is certified from the group's current epoch (decision M3); the owner's browser shows "waiting for keys" or "waiting for you to verify", adds only a verified invitee, only for a statement one of its devices signed, from a claim it keeps until the Add lands, after checking each KeyPackage's account, device, leaf certification and hash, in one Add Commit with the statement's role that carries the Welcome and the owner's wraps of the new epoch (`addInvitee`, the directory including the claimed leaves). An added device joins by its Welcome (`joinProjectByWelcome`: `beginJoin`, the joined epoch's key archive through the provisional exporter, every earlier archive, `completeJoin`; an inviter or server that omits or alters history is refused and the KeyPackage session restored, #817) and posts its account's own wraps; the account's other devices rejoin by External Commit with the account-wrapped rejoin PSK. A pending Add is never resent without its statement. The sharing dialog's encrypted branch invites (email and role), lists pending invitations with Verify (Settings › Encryption at that person's code comparison) and Revoke, and adds ready invitees; share links and role changes are not offered there yet; Settings › Encryption › Invitations accepts the link, after setup when there are no keys; shared projects' names decrypt on the shelf. The slice 5-2 gap is fixed at its root: an epoch's wraps exist for an account only once one of its devices processed that epoch, and roles come from the epoch's key archive or the device's own session, so a browser whose session was behind a member's External Commit reported the member's version as written without an editing role; a read now catches the device's own session up first (which posts the account's wraps of each epoch) and a key that is truly missing is reported as such. The Worker adds the owner's KeyPackage counts, the group epoch for the invitee and `GET /api/e2ee/invitations/sent`. Plaintext sharing is unchanged.

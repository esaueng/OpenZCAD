Slice 3, tapped holes on M15 thread data (schema v66, up from v65; no kernel change, Remus 2026.1.141). A hole sized as a standard tap drill may store `tapped: { length?, clearance?, hand? }`. After the bores are drilled, each one is threaded by the M15 thread generator (`cutThreadDetailed`, through the `cutResolvedThread` helper the Thread feature now shares). The thread is the standard's size and pitch with the ISO 68-1 internal form, which is also the Unified basic profile, so UNC sizes need no form of their own. It starts at the bore's end nearer the entry face and runs the whole bore unless `length` stops it short, and the tap-drill bore truncates its crest as a Thread's. A hole that sets no clearance reads the L09 document print clearance, exactly as a Thread with the Document fit does: `featureReadsPrintClearance` counts it, so the rebuild cache digests the resolved clearance for it too. Print advice is raised once per hole. Refused by name: tapping a hole that is not a standard tap drill, a hand other than right or left, a non-positive length or a negative clearance, and, through the thread generator's own codes, a thread longer than its bore (per point, `At sketch point <id>: …`, on sketch points). A content gate refuses `tapped` in a document that declares v65 or older.

UI: with For set to Tap drill, the Hole tool's Options offer Thread (Drill only, Tapped) and Thread fit (Document, Exact). A tapped hole wears a Thread arrow beside the bore, standing at the depth until it is dragged short, which then stores the length. A History edit reopens with the thread as stored, its hand included, and Drill only clears it. History reads "M6×1 tapped × 10 mm".

Evidence: `test/tapped-holes.test.ts` (11 cases; each build is checked against an M15 Thread cut into the same untapped tap-drill bore, to the volume: an M6 through hole; a blind M6 thread stopped 5 mm from the entry, its thread faces all within those 5 mm and the plain bore below; the document print clearance of 0.1 read as a Document-fit Thread reads it, and an Exact fit ignoring it; 1/4-20 UNC in an inch document; two tapped bores on sketch points taking twice one's material; a thread longer than its bore refused by the generator; the contract, print-clearance read and v66 gate); `apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/tools.test.ts` and `edit.test.ts`; `test/e2e/tapped-holes.spec.ts`, which clicks the block's top face, chooses ISO metric, M6, Tap drill and Tapped, drags the Thread arrow short of the blind bore and checks the stored hole and its History row.

Drawing and flattened-view DXF export ([design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#drawing-dxf-export-d04)): Export DXF writes a drawing sheet — its views at the sheet's scale, hidden lines, sections, dimensions with SOLID arrowheads, notes and the title block — as a DXF R12 drawing in paper millimetres, one layer per line kind (`VISIBLE`, `HIDDEN` with an ISO 128 hidden line type, `DIMENSION`, `CENTER` with the long-dash-dot type, `HATCH`, `CUTTING`, `BORDER`, `THIN`, `TEXT`), deterministic and refused under the same conditions as PDF and SVG. Curves are the page's polylines (exact edges sampled to the display tolerance), labelled as such. The export dialog gains the sketch DXF entry, writing one saved sketch through the existing exact sketch exporter. The R12 writer gains SOLID and the HIDDEN and CENTER line types, leaving flat-pattern files byte-identical. Evidence: `test/drawing-dxf-export.test.ts` (every page stroke and text on its layer, two arrowheads, the dimension's text at its page position, Ø escaped, deterministic bytes, read back through the app's DXF importer with its text counted, the file name, the unchanged DASHED table and SOLID corner order), `ImageExportDialog.test.tsx` (the sketch DXF option) and `test/e2e/drawing-dxf-export.spec.ts` (a dimensioned sheet downloaded as `Plate-Sheet-1.dxf` with its layers, arrowheads and text).

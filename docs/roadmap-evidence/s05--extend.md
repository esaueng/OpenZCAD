Delivered: Extend, Trim's counterpart in the "More modify tools" fold (and an unbound `sketch-extend` shortcut). A click on a line or arc lengthens it from the end nearer the click to the first curve its continuation meets: a line along its own direction, an arc round its own circle (either end). It keeps the entity's identity and its far end, is one undoable transaction per click and stays armed like Trim. The moved end drops the relations that held it where it was (coincident, lock, distance, a line's equal-length and midpoint relations), and the status line counts them. Untouched relations stay, and the new end is held on the curve it reached. It refuses by name, changing nothing: a circle or full arc (no end), nothing beyond the end, composites, text, ellipses, splines, linked projections, and an end a reference dimension measures. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/modify.test.ts` (line end and start, arc both ways against closed-form angles, dropped-relation count, every refusal, undo), `test/sketch-modify-tools.test.ts` (on the pinned kernel the extended line and arc ends follow their boundaries when those move), `edits.test.ts`, `SketchToolRail.test.tsx`, and `test/e2e/sketch-modify-tools.spec.ts` (two clicks extend a line past one vertical and then to the next, the bare end is refused, and two undos restore the original).

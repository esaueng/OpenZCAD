Detail views (schema v78, built on v77; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#detail-views-d03-schema-v78)): the Detail view tool (K) circles a region of a view by a drag from its centre and places it, enlarged at its own scale (by default the smallest preset at least twice the sheet's), anywhere on the sheet. The detail looks as its source does (orientation and bodies follow it) with its own hidden-line toggle; what it draws, offers to pick and points leaders at is cut to its circle; the source shows the circle and letter and the detail its boundary and `DETAIL A (4:1)` caption, through the one page model to preview, PDF, SVG and DXF. Dimensions on a detail read true model values. The panel edits its scale and radius; deleting the source deletes its details. A content gate refuses details below v78. Evidence: `test/drawing-detail-views.test.ts` (validation by name and the shared label sequence; the drag → region → draft at twice the sheet's scale; the transform and footprint; on a kernel box the detail's strokes inside its circle with the corner's edge magnified 4×, the caption and letter in SVG, picking lines cut to the circle and a 40 mm dimension on the detail; circle clipping; scale/radius update, the source's bodies, delete with undo and the v75 gate) and `test/e2e/drawing-detail-views.spec.ts` (a circle dragged on the front view's corner, placed by a click, captioned, exported and reloaded).

Exact curved and helical sweeps (CAD-003), with no faceted fallback. A new Sweep is built by the kernel's exact chain sweep (`sweepExact`, Remus `exact_sweep`) and saved with `construction: 'in-place'`: the profile stays where it is and is carried by the rigid motions of its path, a line moving it along the line and an arc turning it about the arc's axis, which along a tangent-continuous chain is exactly the rotation-minimizing frame. Each profile edge sweeps into one face per path segment, a plane, cylinder, cone or torus where one exists and otherwise an exact rational NURBS; nothing is sampled. A corner between two straight runs is mitred exactly on the bisector plane (corner type: mitre). A closed chain that brings the profile back builds a ring. Refused by name, building nothing and never falling back to facets: a path curve other than a line or arc, a corner next to an arc or one that turns back, a run too short for its mitres, a profile that reaches an arc's axis, one that is not transversal to the path or that would meet itself, and a guide rail (a rail has no exact construction). `solid_volume` reads these bodies off certified face integrals. A new helical sweep was already wound in place (`helicalSweepInPlace`). Saved sweeps and helices keep replaying their original sampled call, unchanged; Diagnostics still names them faceted and now says how to rebuild them, and Edit offers Build: Exact on exactly those, which rewinds or resweeps them from exact surfaces (a helix's coil then starts at the profile). Evidence: `test/exact-sweep.test.ts` (the QA elbow as two planes and one torus at the torus-segment volume with no faceting advisory, a line–arc–line bend that is cylinder, torus, cylinder in any pick order, a rectangle kept flat and round through the bend and round-tripped through STEP with its cylinders intact, a round tube mitred at a square corner, its two cylinders meeting on an exact ellipse, at area × centre-line length, the corner-next-to-an-arc refusal, a bend tighter than the profile refused, the guide refused), `test/sweep-faceting-disclosure.test.ts` (in-place sweeps and helices are not called faceted; saved sampled ones name the rebuild) and `test/e2e/exact-sweeps.spec.ts` (a profile and an L path picked in place build two cylinders and two planes at π·4·35 mm³; a saved sampled helix reopened, switched to Build: Exact and its height dragged, rebuilt in place at its Pappus volume). Open: sweep twist, scale along the path, profile position and orientation options, a round corner, splines in the path, a corner next to an arc, guide rails on the exact route; loft guide curves, connection-point mapping, periodic lofts and end tangency.

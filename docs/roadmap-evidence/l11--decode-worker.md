Phase 4 groundwork, the decode worker (ADR-024, Decompression limits): a lazily started module Worker (`apps/web/src/worker/e2eeDecodeWorker.ts`) decrypts a project envelope of a given kind under that kind's plaintext ceiling (documents and share snapshots `MAX_CLOUD_PROJECT_DOCUMENT_BYTES`, the others `PLAINTEXT_CEILINGS`), decodes the UTF-8 a slice at a time through `JsonComplexityGuard` before `JSON.parse`, then runs `documentStructureError`; and it stream-decrypts a content blob under its kind's ceiling (128 MiB for imported sources, mesh payloads and exports; smaller for thumbnails), returning transferable 1 MiB pieces or a Blob only once the whole blob has authenticated. A declared size over the ceiling, a body that inflates past its declared size, or one that inflates to anything else is refused with the crypto package's `E2eeError` codes. The client (`apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/decodeWorker.ts`) runs one job at a time under a wall-clock budget: a job past it is rejected (`timeout`), the worker is terminated rather than awaited, and the next job runs on a fresh worker; a refusal or a crashed worker never wedges the queue, and callers keep their last good version. Nothing calls it yet. The worker chunk is listed in `LAZY_ENTRY_PATTERNS`, so the launcher can never preload it. Evidence: `test/e2ee-decode-jobs.test.ts` (a gzip bomb with a small declared size, too short and too long bodies, declared sizes over the document, measurements and thumbnail ceilings, JSON too deep and with too many values refused before parsing, a truncated final chunk, valid document and multi-chunk blob round trips), `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/decodeWorker.test.ts` (a fake Worker: overrun jobs terminate their worker and the next job runs on a new one, budgets start on dispatch, refusals keep the worker, crashes and unexpected replies replace it, dispose rejects every pending job) and `test/bundle-size-policy.test.ts`.

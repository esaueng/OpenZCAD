A movable pivot handle in the gizmo (no schema or kernel change: the Move's existing v22 `pivot`). The Move bar gains a Pivot toggle. While it is on, dragging an arrow or the centre ball carries the point the rings turn about and leaves the body where it is: the drag splits into the pivot's own move Rᵀ·Δ and the translation that keeps the body still, so the gizmo lands where it was dragged, and the centre ball snaps to the moving body's own corners, midpoints and centres (read where the preview shows the body) as well as other bodies'. Options show the pivot (pX/pY/pZ, typed values keep the body still by t′ = t + (R − I)(p′ − p)) and "Pivot to body centre"; Done stores the placed pivot, so the turn and any later edit go about it; switching the target body clears it. Evidence: `packages/viewport/src/gizmo/moveTransform.test.ts` (relocating a pivot under no, a 90° and a compound turn leaves three body corners in place; a pivot drag moves the gizmo by exactly the drag and keeps the body still), `DirectModelingOverlays.test.tsx` and the Chromium smoke case "the pivot handle places the turn centre without moving the body" in `test/e2e/transform-pivot.spec.ts` (drag the centre ball under Pivot, Done stays off with Move X/Y at 0, then a 90° turn stores the dragged pivot in History).

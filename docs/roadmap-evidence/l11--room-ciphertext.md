Phase 4, slice 4-9, the collaboration room relays ciphertext for encrypted projects: `ProjectCollaborationRoom` gains a format-2 mode (`packages/cloudflare-adapters/src/e2ee-room.ts`). It keeps the latest signed document envelope and up to 20 older ones, each with its account log record, under keys of their own (`room:e2ee:meta`, `room:e2ee:v:<hash>`; never `room:latest`), reads only envelope headers through an account seam the Worker supplies (`apps/web/worker/e2eeRoom.ts`, the room class the Worker now exports), and never parses or normalizes content. It orders a submission by its header's previous-version hash and document version: one that builds on the head under the group's current key epoch (read from `e2ee_project_groups`) is written to the account through slice 4-5's own `writeVersion` (same version log, fences, signer check and quota; named saves through the revision path with a revision id, never `storeCollaborationCheckpoint`), bound to the session the socket or request was admitted under by its token hash, and only then acknowledged (`e2ee-ack`) and relayed (`e2ee-document`); one that builds on an older version gets `e2ee-conflict` with the latest and, when the room still holds it, the base, as ciphertext; an older key epoch is refused (`epoch-stale`). The account's log stays the one chain: the room adopts the account's head whenever it moved outside the room. A plaintext frame to a format-2 room and an envelope to a plaintext room are refused (`format-mismatch`), and a room that holds plaintext never turns encrypted. The 4-4 Commit route and 4-5's document and revision writes notify the room (`/_internal/e2ee-notice`), which broadcasts `e2ee-epoch` or the new head. The Worker opens the room for a format-2 project only to its owner and only while `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED` is on (ticket, socket, HTTP and the 4-1 format gate); members stay refused until Phase 5, and the room still needs `PROJECT_PERSONAL_SYNC_ENABLED` (or sharing) as before. In the browser, `useCollaboration` takes an `encrypted` codec (`apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/encryptedRoomCodec.ts`, loaded lazily) that seals with `encryptedProjectStore`'s encrypt/sign path in the decode worker and reads every relayed head as a current head (the account's log from the pin, author, leaf authority, epoch binding, decrypt and validate in the decode worker, pin forward); the hook's merge, kept-version and refusal semantics stay Phase 1's (#831/#841), and "Keep my version" seals the kept document on the room's head. `App.tsx` connects owners of encrypted projects, treats a room-acknowledged version as the account's, and lets autosave take over when the room stops carrying edits. Plaintext rooms are unchanged. No new migration; the flag stays off. Evidence: `test/e2ee-room.test.ts` (two Node devices of one owner with the in-process MLS engine against the real Worker, its room class and SQLite: relay through the account with pins moving; a stale edit merged from the returned base and latest; an old-epoch head refused after a Commit whose epoch the room broadcast, landing after catch-up; a named save through the revision path; owner-only and flag-off refusals; a tampered relayed head refused with the pin kept), `test/collaboration-room-socket.test.ts` and `test/cloudflare-adapters.test.ts` (ordering, conflict with base, epoch refusal, plaintext and envelope mismatches, epoch and head notices, storage keys, the HTTP named save, ledger retention), `apps/web/src/lib/useCollaborationEncrypted.test.tsx` (envelopes only, merge and reseal on the latest, reseal after an epoch move, kept versions, refusals, plaintext rooms never load the codec), and `test/e2e/encrypted-room.spec.ts` (two browser contexts of one owner under personal sync in the built app: each browser's real socket reaches the Worker's room class over the encrypted routes' SQLite through Playwright socket routing; an edit on one browser is written to the account by the room, with that browser's autosave standing down, and adopted live on the other; room storage and the account hold ciphertext only).

Phase 5, slice 5-9, share-link crypto, package only (no app, Worker or migration change; nothing is enabled). `packages/crypto/src/share-links.ts` defines the ADR's share-link authority chain, documented byte for byte in the package README: the identity-signed share delegation ("OZS" v1: owner identity hash, sequence, issue and expiry times of at most 30 days apart, and the delegated owner device signing keys in full), the per-link share-authority statement signed by a delegated owner device ("OZA" v1: project, link id, share key hash, mode, owner identity hash, sequence, at most 7 days of validity, signer key hash and the author key hashes allowed to publish snapshots), and owner-signed link grants ("OZL" v1, carrying the key epoch they were made in) and permanent tombstones ("OZY" v1; first drafted as "OZX", which the solo group reset of slice 4-10 already used). Every format is canonical, size-bounded and refused with typed errors; signatures use their own labels. `verifySharedSnapshot` runs the visitor's check in the ADR's order (owner hash, delegation and expiry, delegated signer, statement signature, expiry and share key, named author, snapshot signature and project), `verifyShareLinkSet` checks a document's active set (owner signatures through a caller resolver, project and epoch, tombstones seen or carried, tombstones never dropped, editors carry the set unchanged), and refusals are `ShareLinkError`s with a reason. The document body codec gains version 2, written only when a document carries `shareLinks` (version 1 bytes and every existing vector unchanged; share snapshots never carry the set). The `share-version-key` wrap now binds the snapshot's key epoch too (no code had written one), `shareKeyHash` and `openShareKey` tie a per-epoch `share-key` wrap to its grant, and `sharedProjectSnapshotDocument` moved to `@openzcad/document-core` with a re-export from `@openzcad/persistence`, so callers are unchanged. Evidence: `packages/crypto/src/share-links.test.ts` (expired and not-yet-valid delegations and statements, a delegation over 30 days, forged and undelegated signers, another owner or link, an unnamed or forged snapshot author, another project or kind, tombstoned links, grants for another project or a later epoch, dropped tombstones, editor changes, non-canonical bytes), `vectors.test.ts` (a share key hash, a delegation, a statement, a grant and a tombstone with recorded signatures re-checked with raw WebCrypto, a version 2 body and the `share-key`/`share-version-key` wraps), `fuzz.test.ts`, `bodies.test.ts`, `wrap.test.ts`, `packages/document-core/src/shared-snapshot.test.ts` and `test/shared-snapshot-reexport.test.ts`.

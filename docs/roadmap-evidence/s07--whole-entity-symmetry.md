Whole-entity symmetry (two lines, circles or arcs about a line in one pick sequence). Symmetric now takes two snap points as before, or two whole entities of one kind picked away from their points (the first pick decides, the second follows), then the axis. It writes the existing relations that fix the second entity exactly once on the solver, in one undoable edit that holds the first pick: two lines pair each end with the end nearer its image (two point symmetries), two circles mirror their centres and share a radius, and two arcs mirror the first's start to the second's end and its end to the second's start (reflection reverses an arc) with equal radii, five equations for an arc's five freedoms. Mixed kinds, ellipses, splines, composites, an axis among the mirrored entities and a point after an entity are refused by name. No schema change. Evidence: `test/sketch-constraint-set.test.ts` (on the pinned kernel each set solves without a redundant equation and lands on the closed-form image: a reversed line without flipping, a circle's centre and radius, a quarter arc's reversed sweep), `lib/sketch/constraints.test.ts` (pick kinds, refusals, end pairing) and `test/e2e/sketch-constraint-set.spec.ts` ("Symmetric mirrors a whole line about an axis in one edit", undone in one step).

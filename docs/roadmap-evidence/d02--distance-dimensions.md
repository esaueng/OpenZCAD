Point-to-line, line-to-line and min/max distances (schema v75, built on v74; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#distance-dimensions-d02-schema-v75)): the Distance dimension measures between a point (edge end, circle centre or mark), a straight edge, a straight centreline and a face-on circle — square to a line where there is one, from a line's midpoint between parallel lines, and between centres otherwise — and `extent` measures to circles' near (min) or far (max) sides. It is read from the model on every draw, takes tolerances and affixes, and breaks by name (`not-parallel`, `degenerate`, `foreshortened`, `mark-missing`) rather than rebinding. A content gate refuses it below v75. Evidence: `test/drawing-distance-dimensions.test.ts` (validation by name; on kernel bodies a point to a line, two parallel lines, a centreline to an edge, an edge to a pin's centre, near and far sides with a tolerance, a centre mark to an edge, crossed lines and a point on its line refused, a deleted centreline broken, the aligned layout, the v73 gate) and `test/e2e/drawing-distances.spec.ts` (picked between two edges reading 30, from a centreline to the top edge reading 5, widen → Update → 45, reload).

Stored-unit conversion phase 5 (plan `docs/plans/i03-stored-unit-conversion-plan.md` §5.1–§5.3, §7 row 5): the replayable `units.convert` command (schema v154), payload `{ from, to, factor: [N, D], references }`, each reference entry `{ from, to }`: replay refuses the whole conversion when a place no longer holds its recorded `from`. Applying or replaying it is pure (`applyUnitsConversion`: `convertDocumentUnits`, then `applyReferenceMap` with the recorded map), and older schemas refuse it on the command, the stored log and replay. Authoring needs the kernel: `prepareUnitsConversion` (kernel adapter, run by the disposable `unitsConversionWorker`) runs the transform and field oracle, re-captures references through three cold rebuilds with the geometry and reference oracles, checks that replaying the recorded payload reproduces the verified document byte for byte, and measures the undo entry against `MAX_UNDO_BYTES` with the bound's own measure, refusing a conversion whose undo step the history could not keep even alone (`undo-too-large`) and reporting when it would be the only step left; it returns a preview (counts, rewritten expressions, warnings, undo estimate) or every refusal by node and field, and no command exists until then. `commitUnitsConversion` (web) refuses while other clients are present in the room, for viewers, outside a live room or with an expired or denied edit lease; writes and persists "Before unit conversion (mm → in)" on a fresh revision as a save-state restore does; then re-checks every eligibility condition after that write and executes the command as one undo step. Imported meshes with a referenced payload now convert by recording `payloadUnits` (v153) and leaving the blob alone. The save-state diff shows one "Converted stored units mm → in (n values)" row in place of a row per scaled value. Rounding a ratio of lengths and a pole or domain edge in a sum of lengths are reported as warnings naming the field (0 over 26 conversions of the 15 parity, demo, import-modeling and fixture documents). Parity property (`test/parity/units-convert-command.spec.ts`): the 11 scenarios phase 4 accepts are authored through the kernel and replayed without it to the same document. No UI (phase 6).

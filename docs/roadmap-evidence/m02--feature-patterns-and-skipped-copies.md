Slice 1, feature patterns and per-instance suppression (schema v62, up from v61; no kernel change, Remus 2026.1.141). The `pattern` feature takes two optional fields. `sourceFeatureIds` repeats earlier holes and add/cut extrudes on the target body instead of copying the whole body. `skippedInstances` leaves copies out by their instance label (`"3"`, or `"1-2"` in a grid), so skipping one renames no other copy. A content gate refuses either field on a document that declares an older schema. Validation refuses: the original's label, a malformed or repeated label, an empty list, a later feature, a feature that is not a hole, boss or cut, and a to-face extrude. The build refuses by name a suppressed source, a source not made on the target's body chain, and a Move or Mirror between the source and the pattern. A skipped label that a lowered count no longer reaches is ignored with an advisory.

The build (`packages/kernel-adapter/src/exact-feature-pattern.ts`) makes each feature's tool once at its original place: the hole's bore and counterbore through the shared `holeToolSolids`, or the extrude's swept profile. It repeats the tool through the same K05 arm as the whole-body pattern: the kernel's journaled linear and grid entry points and the closed ring where copies cannot overlap, rigid copies elsewhere. Copies are cut one tool at a time, or fused onto the body for a boss. Through extents reach across the target from wherever each copy lands. Each copy is proven: it must touch and change the body, a hole copy's bores must come back as analytic cylinders on its own axis, and the result must be one valid solid. A copy that misses refuses by its label, with "skip it or change the pattern". Faces the copies leave alone keep their names, and each hole copy's bore is named `pattern.face.instance.<label>.source.<hole feature id>.hole.face.bore`, so two repeated holes never share a name. Whole-body patterns drop skipped copies before the overlap fuse and keep the others' instance names.

UI (U11 pick-and-drag, shared by Linear, Circular and Grid pattern): a Repeat option (Body, Features); in Features a click on a face picks the hole, boss or cut that made it. The spacing arrow and angle rig stand on the picked feature. Every copy wears a chip where it lands, and a click skips it or brings it back. A History edit reopens with the same features and skipped copies, and an untouched edit writes them back unchanged; a repeated feature whose faces the model no longer shows is kept as stored rather than dropped. The History row reads "× 3 · 1 skipped".

Evidence: `test/feature-pattern.test.ts` (22 cases with closed-form volumes and bore positions: a through hole ×5 along X, two holes repeated with distinct copy names, a skipped copy without renaming, a 3 × 2 grid with one skipped, an edited hole the copies follow, a missing copy refused and then skipped, a through-all square cut around a ring, a boss row with one skipped, a moved body refused by name, whole-body skips with names kept, a stale skip advisory, a skipped copy of an overlapping full ring; plus document, command and v62-gate refusals); `apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/tools.test.ts`, `edit.test.ts` and `featureSummary.test.ts`; `test/e2e/feature-pattern.spec.ts`, which picks a hole in place, picks its bore under Repeat Features, drags the spacing, clicks copy 1's chip to skip it, then reopens the pattern from History and clicks the chip to bring the copy back.

Budget: the 10 × 10 grid preview (`node scripts/measure-active1-baselines.mjs --only pattern-node,pattern-browser`, 3 runs each) went from 7,540 ms before to 7,550 ms after in the browser, and from 7,684 ms to 7,631 ms in Node, against the 10,000 ms gate.

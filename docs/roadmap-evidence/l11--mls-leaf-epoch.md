MLS leaf certifications bind the certification epoch (follow-up to slice 3T-2). The leaf certification payload is now version 2: the MLS encoding of the label `openzcad mls leaf certification v2`, project id, user id, device id, cipher suite, leaf key and a u64 `certifiedEpoch`, the group's current epoch when the device certifies (`leafCertificationPayload` and `MlsSession.certificationPayload` take it; version 1 is no longer produced). The app's certification is `u8 2 | u64 certifiedEpoch | r || s`, so the signed epoch travels with the signature in the leaf credential; `certifyLeaf` signs from the epoch it is given (0 when creating a group), and `buildDeviceDirectory` sets `certifiedEpoch` for a leaf key only after the device's signature verifies over that epoch (two verified certifications of one key keep the earlier epoch). Recovery Adds therefore work for a key certified at or after the identity's latest leaf removal instead of always failing closed, and a server that rewrites the epoch breaks the signature. `packages/mls/pkg` was rebuilt with `build.sh` (Rust 1.96.0, wasm-bindgen 0.2.126); the lazy MLS WASM stays inside its 1.5 MiB budget. Evidence: `packages/mls/src/certification.test.ts` (pinned v2 payload vector, one payload per epoch, non-epochs refused), the main scenario step "the leaf certification payload binds the certified epoch" (in-process and Worker protocol), and `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/mls/directory.test.ts` (a certification whose claimed epoch differs from the signed one is refused, a version 1 certification is refused, and the policy accepts a recovery Add of a key certified after the latest removal and refuses one certified before it).

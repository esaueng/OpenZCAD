Slice 3, viewer and commenter roles. Members: owner, editors and viewers all comment (slice 2); the author, an editor or the owner resolves; the author or the owner deletes. Share links: a plaintext link is a viewer link, view or tweak only, as before, unless the owner ticks "Visitors can comment" when creating it, which makes it a commenter link (migration 0059 adds `project_share_links.allow_comments`, off for every existing link; the owner's list names it "Commenter link"). A commenter link's visitor, with no account, opens Comments from File, signs with a typed name (1 to 60 characters) and pins threads or replies; their comments show as "Name (via link)", carry no account and record the link they came through (`project_comments.share_link_id`, set to null if the link row goes). A visitor never resolves or deletes, sees every comment with no member's account id, and posts through `GET/POST /api/share/comments` with the token in `x-openzcad-share-token`, never in the URL. That route applies the link's own read rules (not revoked, within 30 days, project not trashed, owner's sharing on, plaintext only) plus the link's comment flag, answers one opaque 404 otherwise, and is rate limited per address like every public share route. Encrypted links keep view and tweak only until encrypted comments (slice 4). Evidence: `test/share-link-comments.test.ts` (D1: the flag through create, list and the visitor's read; the route signed without an account and recorded against its link, members seeing it, and 404 for a viewer link, an unknown token, a revoked link, a trashed and an encrypted project; the name rule), `ProjectSharingDialog.test.tsx` (a commenter link is minted only when ticked), `CommentsPanel` visitor mode, and the CI smoke case in `test/e2e/project-comments.spec.ts` (a commenter link's visitor signs a comment pinned to the shared model, with no Resolve or Delete).

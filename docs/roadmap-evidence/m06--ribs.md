Ribs (schema v101): a `rib` feature braces a body from an open chain of sketch lines and arcs, `thickness` centred on the profile or to either side. `parallel` fills from the profile, extended along its end tangents, to the body within the sketch plane, the thickness across the plane; `normal` thickens the profile within the plane (offset arcs, mitred line corners) and runs it along the sketch normal to the body. The material is exactly the region the profile and the body close off: the tool is cut by the body into separate regions (`booleanRegions`) and the one beside the profile is kept only when it closes against the body, then united with it through the checked union a Boolean takes, so the result is one body. By default the rib fills the side (or runs the way) that closes, the profile's left (or along the normal) when both do; Flip takes the other. Refused by name, building nothing: a profile in open space ("does not close against the body"), one inside the body, a closed or branching profile, a curve other than a line or arc, a rib thicker than an arc in its profile is tight. Rib runs in place (Edges & faces): its profile is curve clicks, its body a click (a lone body is taken), its thickness an arrow from the profile's middle along the sketch normal; it reopens from History. Evidence: `test/rib.test.ts` (the v101 round trip and v100 refusal, an exact gusset across a bracket's inside corner at 7 500 + 625 mm³, a thickness edit to 1 250 mm³, the flipped side refused, a half-circle arch over a U bracket kept by its crown at 5 000 + 2 × (π·17²/2 + 150) mm³ with its cylinder face, a gusset whose coordinates are expressions, a normal rib from a sketch above a block down to its top, open-space and buried profiles refused) and `test/e2e/rib.spec.ts` (the profile clicked, the lone bracket taken, the thickness dragged to about 4, one body at 7 500 + 312.5 × thickness mm³).

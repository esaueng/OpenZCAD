Slice 1, drawings on share links with a PDF download (plan: docs/plans/l08-l03-review-and-versions-plan.md). A share link still serves the current model only, and drawing sheets stay with the project unless the owner ticks them under Share › Drawings on links, which says that title blocks show as drawn (author, date, drawing number). The choice is a document field, `shareLinkSheetIds`, kept outside `drawings` so no sheet record or reader changes, and written as a forward write ("Choose drawings for share links": one revision, no undo entry) that syncs like any edit; no schema version (an older client keeps the list and shares no sheets). `sharedProjectSnapshotDocument` keeps exactly the chosen sheets, whole, in sheet order, and drops the list itself; a deleted sheet drops out and an empty choice removes the field. A visitor opens Draw on the link read only ("Drawings on a shared link are read only — Make a copy to edit them": no new sheet, no annotation tool) and exports each sheet as PDF, SVG or DXF in the browser. A link that carries no sheet keeps Draw locked ("This link shares no drawings"). E2EE: the choice and the sheets travel inside the document and the encrypted share snapshot every editor's browser republishes (ADR-024); no envelope, grant or route changes. Evidence: `test/share-link-sheets.test.ts` (only chosen sheets, sheet order, duplicates and unknown ids dropped, forward write, cleared field, deletion, undo of a model edit keeps the choice, malformed list dropped on load, accepted by the account's document check), `packages/document-core/src/shared-snapshot.test.ts`, `ShareLinkDrawings.test.tsx`, and the CI smoke case `test/e2e/share-link-drawings.spec.ts` (an owner's sheet of a 40 × 20 × 10 box, unchosen in the snapshot; chosen, a visitor with no account opens it in Draw read only and downloads a `%PDF-` file).

Parts lists and balloons (schema v80, built on v78; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#parts-lists-and-balloons-d02-schema-v80)), built from the AS03 BOM table model (`assemblyBomTable`): the Parts list tool (P) places the assembly's BOM table on the paper — a ruled table with a bold header, the chosen BOM columns (default item, quantity, part) and numeric columns aligned to their end — and the Balloon tool (O) names a picked part's item number in a circle with a leader to its edge. Rows, cells and numbers are read from the table each time the sheet is drawn, so the drawing, the Assemble panel and the CSV agree; a list with no assembly (`no-bom`), a balloon whose part left the assembly (`not-in-bom`) and one on an edge of a part in another row (`not-on-part`) break by name; a re-picked balloon names its new edge's part, and its leader points only at what its view draws of the edge (inside a detail's circle, broken as `leader-hidden` when none shows). Both export through the one page model to PDF, SVG and DXF. A content gate refuses them below v80. Evidence: `test/drawing-bom-balloons.test.ts` (validation by name; on kernel bodies an assembled plate and two copies of a pin listed as two rows, balloons reading 1 and 2 from either pin's component, the ruled layout and SVG text; `no-bom`, `not-in-bom` and `not-on-part`; a leader kept to the drawn pieces of its edge and broken when the view draws none; chosen columns in order; drafts from both tools, a balloon re-picked onto a pin naming the pin's item, reload and the v78 gate) and `test/e2e/drawing-bom-balloons.spec.ts` (an imported assembly's drawing gets a parts list and a balloon reading 1, exported and reopened).

A checked fillet can reuse the dense display mesh prepared for its preview when the mesh buffers, face ranges and hashes, edge IDs and adjacent faces match. On a 32 × 20 × 12 mm box with a 1 mm fillet on all twelve edges, median Apply to the committed frame fell from 186 to 158 ms for immediate Apply and from 99 to 83 ms after validation had settled. All six pairs improved for each timing on an RTX 3090 with OpenZCAD control 41f34fe3c and Remus c81a719a. Preview and commit had identical mesh buffers, and Undo restored the original box in all 36 browser sessions.

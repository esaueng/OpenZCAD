Pattern by total extent and uniform orientation. While Linear pattern is armed, a Spacing · Extent choice rides beside its slot: Extent reads the typed distance as running from the source to the last instance, so the instances share it evenly (an extent prefilled with the direction line's length spreads them along it). While Circular pattern is armed, a Rotate · Uniform choice does the same: Uniform carries each instance round the pivot without turning it, so a line's midpoint or a circle's or arc's centre lands where the turn takes the source's, and a uniform line is held parallel as well as equal to its source. The setting is read when the picks complete and travels with the values, so a change mid-entry cannot reinterpret them; absent settings are the step-spaced, rotated patterns of before. Refusals are by name: a zero extent, an extent too short for its steps, overlapping steps, and a uniform pattern anchored on the pivot. No schema change. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/modify.test.ts` (extent steps, refusals, uniform lines and arcs against closed-form positions, the setting carried through both value steps), `test/sketch-modify-tools.test.ts` (on the pinned kernel uniform instances follow the source's turn and length without redundancy; an extent pattern solves like its step equivalent), `SketchToolRail.test.tsx`, `machine.test.ts` and `test/e2e/sketch-modify-tools.spec.ts` ("Linear pattern spreads its instances over an extent").

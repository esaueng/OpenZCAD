Auxiliary views (schema v81, built on v80; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#auxiliary-views-d03-schema-v81)): the Auxiliary view tool (U) picks a straight edge a front, top or side view draws true length and inclined, and a click on one side places a view seen square to it, so an inclined face is drawn true size. The view stores its frame (direction of sight and page-right, square to each other and to the source's sight), keeps its source's orientation and bodies, is placed freely with an arrow and letter beside its source and a `VIEW A` caption, and reads true lengths in its plane; one `drawingViewFrame` now serves every projection, measurement and pick. Horizontal or vertical edges are refused (a principal view already sees them). A content gate refuses it below v81. Evidence: `test/drawing-auxiliary-views.test.ts` (frame and source validation by name; on a box turned 30° about Y the sloping edge picked, the frame square to it with page-up the front's sight, the face projected true size 20 × 10, a 20 mm dimension on the auxiliary view, the VIEW A caption and arrow; bodies following the source, delete and the v80 gate) and `test/e2e/drawing-auxiliary-views.spec.ts` (an imported tilted block, its sloping edge picked in the front view, VIEW A placed, exported and reopened).

Wall-thickness analysis (view state only, no schema change), on the same `surface-analysis` query path and overlay as draft, curvature and zebra (`exact-surface-analysis.ts`; one engine with the clash query), for 3D-print and moulding checks. Display › Analysis › Thickness measures by the ray method: from the centre of each triangle of a per-face mesh subdivided by longest-edge bisection (so samples stand away from edges), projected onto the exact surface, along the inward exact normal to the first wall the ray meets. Planes, cylinders and spheres are crossed in closed form (`getAnalyticSurfaceParams`), each crossing confirmed on the trimmed face by the kernel's exact face distance; cones, tori and NURBS are sphere-traced on exact point-to-face distances (a step that size cannot cross a wall) up to the nearest closed-form crossing, and a trace that cannot rule out an earlier wall refuses the sample rather than report the crossing. Every figure is labelled approximate, with the body's resolution (1e-4 of its size) shown, except a flat face whose every sample lands on a parallel opposing plane (`getOpposingPlanarFacePairs`), which reads as their exact separation; the panel counts exact face ranges and names the thinnest wall, exact or approximate, and the faces under the minimum wall (default 1 mm in the document's units; the minimum only recolours: red under it, amber under twice it, green above). Limits stated on screen: thin spots between samples, thickness other than along the normal (the ray method reads sharp inside corners thick), and samples a ray cannot start or finish (knife edges, shown grey and listed); bodies over 40,000 samples are refused by name.

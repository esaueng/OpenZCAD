Stepped sections (schema v93, built on v92; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#stepped-sections-d03-schema-v93)): an aligned section's cutting line steps to other depths along its run (`DrawingSectionCut.steps`, edited in the section's Properties). The geometry worker removes one strip per segment with exact axis-aligned box booleans, the shallower strip's box running past each step so only the deeper one makes the jog face; every segment's cut face is hatched, the section view draws no line where it steps (ISO 128-44) while the jog's edges stay dimensionable, and the line on the source jogs, thick at its ends and every change of direction. A cut the kernel refuses refuses the view by name. A content gate refuses steps below v93. Evidence: `test/drawing-stepped-sections.test.ts` (validation by name, the step axis and worker cut, the plane key, edit and removal commands, the v93 gate; on a kernel block cut at z = 3 left of x = 20 and z = 7 right of it, two cut faces hatched 800 mm², no drawn edge along the step, a 4 mm jog edge kept for dimensions, the jogged line's corners on the page and six thick strokes) and `test/e2e/drawing-stepped-sections.spec.ts` (a square section across a block's front view given a step to z = 8 in Properties, its line jogging with thick corners, hatched, exported to SVG and reopened). Still open: lineage for cut edges, and exact cuts the pinned kernel refuses.

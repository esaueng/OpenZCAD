Second slice: guidelines, guidepoints, off-plane projection and snap hints. Guideline extensions: past either end of a line, the pointer snaps onto its extension and a guideline is drawn from that end. A point placed there is held on the line (an on-curve relation, with Auto-constrain on). Tangent guidelines: while a line is drawn, the two points where it would touch each circle (and those on an arc's sweep) are offered from its anchor, with the guideline drawn. A line ending there keeps a tangent relation. On the pinned kernel a hold on the circle at the exact tangent point adds no rank (its gradient coincides with the tangent's), so the settle solve leaves it out rather than adding a redundant relation. Guidepoints: besides the sketch's own endpoints, midpoints, centres and line crossings, the model's vertices, edge midpoints, circle centres (hole and boss centres) and planar face centres are lifted into the sketch plane as named snaps. They carry no stored point, so they are positions to land on, not relations. Off-plane geometry: model guidepoints on the plane are always offered; those in front of or behind it only while the view looks within 1° of the sketch normal, named "(projected)". Snap hints: a new `sketching.snapHints` setting (Settings and the sketch palette; on by default, as the labels always were) names the engaged snap beside its glyph; off keeps the glyph and the snap. A new sketch's first entity commits without a settling solve, since with nothing drawn before it nothing it infers can be redundant. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/sketch/guides.test.ts` (extension feet and guideline segments past either end only, tangent points square to the radius, arc sweep filtering, none from inside, on-curve and tangent inference from guide snaps, on-plane versus projected model guidepoints and their labels, orthogonal-view test), `test/sketch-inference.test.ts` (pinned kernel: an extension hold and a tangent-point line solve without redundancy and without moving), `test/app-settings.test.ts`, `test/settings-search-index.test.ts`, and `test/e2e/sketch-guides.spec.ts` (a line started on another's extension is held on it and vertical; a line clicked onto a circle's tangent guide is tangent to it; with a box below the plane, its corner is projected in as "Model vertex (projected)", a line starts exactly on it, and Snap hints off drops the label but keeps the snap).

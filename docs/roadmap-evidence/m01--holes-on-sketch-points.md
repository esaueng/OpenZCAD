Slice 1, multi-position holes on sketch points (schema v64, up from v63; no kernel change, Remus 2026.1.141). A `hole` may store `points`: an earlier sketch and the I01 point entities it is drilled at. Each point is read on its sketch plane, which must be parallel to the entry face, and dropped onto the face along its normal, so a sketch on the face or on an offset plane places its bores the same way; `position` is then stored but not read. Every bore takes the hole's size, style and depth (a through bore clears the body from its own entry point), is cut and proven one at a time as a single hole is, and is named by its point (`hole.face.at.<point id>.bore` and `.counterbore`; shoulders and floors that share a plane stay unnamed, as a pattern's copies do). A moved point carries its bore. Refused by name: a missing sketch, a later sketch, an entity that is not a point, an empty or repeated list, a deleted point, a sketch that is not parallel to the face, and a point whose bore misses the body (`At sketch point <id>: …`). Clearing `points` returns the hole to its one stored position. An M02 feature pattern repeats such a hole whole, each copy's bores named by copy and point. History makes the sketch a parent of the hole, and an edit to the sketch revalidates it. A content gate refuses holes on sketch points in a document that declares v63 or older.

UI (U11 pick-and-drag): the Hole tool's Position gains Sketch points. After the face, clicks on a sketch's points (drawn as small crossed marks) choose where bores go; the diameter handle stands on the first point and sizes every bore. A History edit reopens with the points as picks and writes them back unchanged; switching back to Clicked or Typed clears them. History reads "⌀ 6 mm through at 3 points".

Evidence: `test/hole-points.test.ts` (17 cases on the pinned kernel: through bores at four points with closed-form volume, axes and per-point names; blind counterbores at two points; points dropped from a parallel sketch on the plate's bottom; a moved point carried and a deleted point refused; a point off the face refused by its id; a non-parallel sketch refused; switching between one position and points; an M02 linear feature pattern repeating a two-point hole to six bores; the document, ordering and v64-gate refusals); `apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/tools.test.ts`, `edit.test.ts`, `featureHistory.test.ts`, `affectedFeatureTargets.test.ts`; `test/e2e/hole-points.spec.ts`, which picks the plate's top face, sets Position to Sketch points, clicks three sketch points, drags the diameter handle and checks the stored hole and its History row.

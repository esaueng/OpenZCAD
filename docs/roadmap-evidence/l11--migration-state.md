Phase 7, slice 7-1, migration state and the server's role in moving an existing plaintext project to encrypted storage, behind the new off-by-default `E2EE_MIGRATION_ENABLED` flag (only for accounts encrypted projects are open to; reported in `/api/e2ee/config` as `migrationEnabled` only once open and migration 0042 is installed). D1 migration `0042_e2ee_project_migrations.sql` adds `e2ee_project_migrations` (one row per project: direction `encrypt` or `decrypt`, state `staging`, `cutover`, `purging`, `done` or `abandoned` moving only along that machine, owner, device, a 32-byte lease with its expiry, the plaintext head and measurement revision staging began from, the staged head, the cutover's counts, a purge cursor and purge counts; project and user cascades, erasure fences that let profile erasure only forget the device and lease) and replaces migration 0033's version quota and 0034's blob accounting triggers so a format-1 project's staged ciphertext counts toward nothing until the cutover (decision M10); before 0042 every encrypted row belongs to a format-2 project, so their answers are unchanged. `apps/web/worker/e2eeProjectMigrations.ts` serves `/api/e2ee/projects/:id/migration` (`GET`; `POST` start, owner only, a live plaintext project with no members or pending invitations (decision M7) and nothing staged left over, or a resume once the lease lapsed; `…/lease` renew; `…/cutover`; `…/abandon`; `…/purge`). While a project is staged its plaintext writes, named saves, duplicates, sharing, uploads and room (tickets and sockets; the start also closes the room's connections) answer 409 `PROJECT_MIGRATING` through the format gate, and reads keep working; the encrypted-project routes of slices 4-4 to 4-7 (group create and Commits, wraps, versions, revisions, project info, content blobs and their upload sessions, measurements and view state) take ciphertext for that format-1 project only from the owner's device holding the live lease (`x-e2ee-migration-lease`, `e2eeMigrationLease.ts`), every write fencing on the lease in its own SQL; staged versions move the migration row's staged head, never `projects`, and the first builds on nothing. The cutover is one D1 batch that checks what the server can see (every retained plaintext revision id has a staged named save whose body is retained, decision M9; a staged head newer than the plaintext head, with its group and project info; the plaintext head and measurements unchanged since staging began; still no members or pending invitations), flips `storage_format` to 2 at the staged head, revokes plaintext share links and unaccepted invitations (decision M8) and starts counting the staged blobs as artifact usage. Abandon unlocks the plaintext at once and drops the staged rows, upload sessions and objects a bounded step per call. Purge, driven by the owner's browser and finished by the hourly scheduled sweep for one left idle, deletes a bounded step per call and resumes from its cursor: the room's plaintext keys through a new internal room endpoint, the row's name and document, revisions, R2 documents, assets and artifacts (keeping any object another project's row still names), plaintext upload sessions, measurements and workspace sessions; share links serve a projection built from the deleted document and are revoked. The server never transforms content. `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/projectMigrationsApi.ts` adds the thin client and `withMigrationLease`; the engine and UI are slice 7-2. Ordinary traffic does not depend on 0042 or the flag: off, no statement names the table; without the migration only a cached probe does. The flag stays off; no migration was applied remotely and no real project was migrated. Evidence: `test/e2ee-project-migrations.test.ts` (Node devices with the in-process MLS engine against the real Worker, its room class and SQLite: a lease conflict and hand-over with the old holder's writes, renewal and cutover refused; staging only by the lease holder, a lease lapsing between check and write landing nothing; `PROJECT_MIGRATING` for plaintext writes, named saves, room ticket and socket, share links, measurements and uploads while reads work; cutover refused until every save state is staged with its body, when the plaintext moved, when shared, and for a head not newer than the plaintext's; an atomic cutover rolled back whole by an injected failure, then flipping the format and revoking a link and an invitation, opened by a fresh device; abandon resuming after a failed delete, unlocking at once and dropping every staged row and object; purge resuming after an interruption and leaving no plaintext in D1, R2 or the room; an object another project names kept and a copy still working; the M10 quota exemption for versions and blobs and counting from the cutover on; profile and full erasure, recycle-bin purge and the sweep; flag off, and migrations only through 0041), `apps/web/src/lib/e2ee/projectMigrationsApi.test.ts`, `test/cloudflare-adapters.test.ts` and `test/d1-migration-split.test.ts`.

Slice 3 (no schema change): the in-place **Sheet metal** and **Bend** tools. Sheet metal (From sketch, beside Extrude) takes one or more sketch cells as Extrude does and makes the flat sheet with no bends: thickness is a drag handle and chip; side, material, the material's gauge table (a gauge sets the thickness in document units and is recorded only while the thickness still is that gauge), inside radius (follows T until set), K-factor or DIN 6935, and default relief sit under Options, with a readout of the rule and the material's rule-of-thumb minimum radius. Defaults: mild steel, 1.5 mm (16 ga in inch documents), R = T, K 0.44, rectangular relief. Bend (Edges & faces; also what History reopens a sheet-metal feature with) offers the straight lines, construction lines included, of sketches on the blank's plane; a click adds a bend row (90°, up, centre line), a second click or the row's delete removes it; the bend table edits angle, up/down, position, radius, K and relief overrides, with expressions kept as typed; a click on a flat region sets the fixed point; a fold slider (0–100 %) shows the sheet folded that far through the adapter's `sheetMetalFoldPreview` (worker request `sheet-metal-fold`), starting flat; Done commits one `updateSheetMetal`, and refusals and advisories show in the bar. History label "1.5 mm mild steel, 3 bends", History icon, selection filters, palette and keymap reach. Evidence: `apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/sheetMetal.test.ts` (requests, gauges, Options rows, untouched edits written back as stored with expressions, row changes, line candidates, fixed-point picks) and `test/e2e/sheet-metal.spec.ts` (blank and line → Sheet metal → Bend → line → Done gives the 50 × 50 L; reopened from History and turned down; undo and redo).

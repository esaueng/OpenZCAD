Stored-unit conversion now accepts variable fillets, including per-corner radii (v151), in both mm→in and mm→cm. Oracle 2 refused them on area: the kernel measures B-spline face area by tessellating at a fixed 0.08 model-unit deflection, which isn't comparable across units, even re-measured in one kernel (7.4% apart for control nets equal to 3e-17). Such faces now leave the area total, and each one is matched one to one. The source body is serialized and scaled into the verification kernel. The two faces must have the same NURBS structure and exactly the same normalized knots. Their distance is bounded over the whole surface by the largest pole difference plus 2δ/(1−δ) × the pole reach, where δ is the largest relative difference between the normalized weights. Each trimmed face must also lie on the other at sampled points (`pointToFaceDistance`). A body whose B-spline faces cannot be compared refuses by name and never falls back to area. A 1% end-radius or interior-station change is refused at 0.035 mm or 0.025 mm (`test/units-reference-recapture.test.ts`). The NURBS import still refuses on volume (`test/parity/unit-conversion.spec.ts`).

2026-11-05 sphere-patch identity. Remus builds a primitive sphere from two hemispheres trimmed at the equator. Both share one exact witness, so their ADR-011 hashes stay identical (unchanged). Each is now named `primitive.sphere.face.z-max` / `z-min` by the pole its trimmed face holds. The kernel's trim-aware `pointToFaceDistance` puts a pole at 0 from its own patch and √2·R from the other, so no witness, schema or kernel change was needed. Anything short of one pole per patch names neither. When a rigid transform or a boolean's operand copy leaves the two patches tied on witness, the tie is settled by carrying a clean pole through the known transform: exactly one tied result face must hold it and every other must be clearly off it. Otherwise the split stays unnamed. Boolean face evolution verifies sphere faces against the kernel's exact sphere, measured alongside the witness and not persisted. A generated seam lying on a sphere is checked against that sphere too. When the kernel cuts one face pair's intersection loop into several pieces around a cylinder wall, each piece is named `…between.<pair>.piece.i.of.n` by its midpoint angle about the wall axis, measured from the wall's single seam line. This follows the existing straight-edge piece rule. It fails closed with no single seam, a midpoint within 1e-3 rad of the seam, tied angles, or a piece off the wall. Result: on the sphere ∪ off-axis boss (`test/support/sphere-cylinder-union.ts`), both sphere faces and all four seam pieces are named. The names survive undo/redo and a JSON reopen on a fresh adapter. A seam fillet picked with lineage demand keeps resolving after the Cylinder radius changes from 6 to 7. A hash-only pick of the same seams still reports "A selected edge no longer exists". Because every pick is referenced, the diameter drag may now cross the union to the source Cylinder. Imported STEP spheres stay unnamed (no feature contract; twin witnesses publish nothing). Evidence: `test/sphere-patch-lineage.test.ts`, `packages/kernel-adapter/src/exact-sphere-patches.test.ts`. Remaining: the cylinder wall's own seam line comes back `unresolved` from the kernel's evolution and stays hash-only; the sphere's equator segments stay hash-only by design; spheres made by other features (revolve, imports) are not named.

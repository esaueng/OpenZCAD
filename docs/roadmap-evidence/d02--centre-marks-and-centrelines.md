Centre marks, intersection marks and centrelines (schema v73, built on v72; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#centre-marks-intersection-marks-and-centrelines-d02-schema-v73)): a centre mark on a face-on circle, an intersection mark where two straight edges' lines cross, and 2-point, 2-line (midway between parallel edges) and 3-point circular centrelines, each read from the model whenever the sheet is drawn and drawn as ISO 128 chain lines run 2.5 mm past what they mark. A centre or intersection mark serves as a dimension and centreline point (`anchor: 'mark'`); deleting it leaves what measured to it broken (`mark-missing`) until re-picked, never rebound. Tools: Centre mark (M), Intersection mark (X), Centreline (L) and Circular centreline (B), made as soon as their picks are in. A content gate refuses them below v73. Evidence: `test/drawing-centerlines.test.ts` (validation by name; on kernel bodies a pin's centre mark, a box corner's intersection, the midline between its top and bottom edges, the circle through three corners, a centreline from a mark and a horizontal dimension from a mark; `mark-missing`, `parallel`, `not-parallel` refusals; chain lengths past the rim and box; SVG; one undoable command and the v70 gate), `DrawingWorkspace.test.tsx` (an intersection mark made from two picks with no placement click) and `test/e2e/drawing-centerlines.spec.ts` (a centreline between two edges, an intersection mark, a horizontal dimension from the mark reading 30, SVG chain lines, reload).

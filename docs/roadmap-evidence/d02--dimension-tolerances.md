Tolerances, prefixes and suffixes (schema v68, built on v67; [design](docs/plans/drawing-mvp-design.md#dimension-tolerances-prefixes-and-suffixes-d02-schema-v68)): a dimension takes a symmetric (`20 ±0.05`), deviation (`+0.10` over `-0.05`, stacked), limits (`20.10` over `19.95`) or basic (framed) tolerance, and a prefix and suffix (`2× … TYP`). Tolerances are stored as deviations from the measured value, so limits follow the model through the explicit Update (a box widened from 30 to 45 reads `45.10/44.95`). Amounts are stated to at most six decimals, so a stored tolerance never prints as zero; a reference dimension, a chamfer and a counterbore or countersink callout take none; a re-pick keeps the tolerance, prefix and suffix; a toleranced diameter on an M01 hole's rim states the diameter instead of the hole's callout. The stacked pair and the basic frame go through the one page model to preview, PDF and SVG; a sheet without them lays out byte-identical. A content gate refuses the fields below v68. Evidence: `test/drawing-dimension-tolerances.test.ts` (validation by name including sub-print-precision amounts, re-pick keeping the format, each format's text, limits read from the kernel body before and after a widen, stacked/framed layout, PDF/SVG contents and byte-stable reload, the v67 gate), `test/hole-callouts.test.ts` (toleranced bore diameter), `DrawingWorkspace.test.tsx` (stacked drawing, tolerance kind switch, refused upper below lower, prefix/suffix, Reference drops the tolerance) and `test/e2e/drawing-tolerances.spec.ts` (limits, prefix and suffix on a picked dimension, SVG/PDF, widen → Update → new limits, reload).

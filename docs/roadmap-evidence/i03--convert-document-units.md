Stored-unit conversion phase 2 (plan `docs/plans/i03-stored-unit-conversion-plan.md` §7 row 2): `convertDocumentUnits` in `packages/document-core/src/units-convert.ts` is a pure, tests-only transform between mm, cm and inch. It scales registry lengths and positions by k and areas by k², rewrites expressions by rule D (suffixed and typed-length text unchanged, unit-free text wrapped as `(E) / 25.4` with exact factors and unwrapped on the way back, numerals scaled to the shortest 15–17-digit value that converts back exactly), converts typed-length parameters and every configuration row, recomputes parameter values and drops `derived`. Every expression, parameter and configuration cell is audited structurally: a length that reaches an operation that does not commute with a change of unit (`round`/`floor`/`ceil`, trigonometry, an unclassified function, `^` with a non-integer or length exponent), or a length in a field or parameter that does not scale, refuses whatever today's value. A field-level oracle then evaluates every classified field and stored literal before and after: no value may change sign or cross zero, lengths and areas agree at relative 1e-12, unscaled values must be bit-identical unless they are ratios of lengths, and whole-number counts stay exact. Scaling never overflows when the true result is finite, and an out-of-range result refuses. The active configuration row's stale cells are replaced by the converted live parameters. It refuses `m`, custom features, imported-mesh payloads and non-empty drawings, PMI, assemblies and reference images; the print clearance converts. Round trips mm→cm/inch→mm restore every converting parity scenario, fixture and the import-modeling corpus byte for byte; Scissors (assembly) and the scripting fixture (custom feature) refuse (`test/units-convert.test.ts`, `test/parity/unit-conversion.spec.ts`). Not wired to a command or UI.

Slice C, scripted custom features (schema v110, builds on v108; v109 is claimed by #1741): a `custom-feature` step embeds a versioned script (`id`, `version`, `api 1`, `source` and its SHA-256 `digest`) and replaces its target body. Scripts are data, not code: `@openzcad/scripting` parses and checks them (typed `input`s, `pick`s that are exact queries on witness data — `type`, `normal`, `axis`, `radius`, `max`, `min` — with a declared count, and `fillet`/`chamfer`/`shell` steps; at most 60 steps, no loops, functions, imports or I/O) and interprets them in the kernel worker's build loop, where each step runs as the ordinary builder under the instance's id on the body the previous step left, its picks re-evaluated on that body on every build. A pick that does not find exactly its count, a digest mismatch, an unknown input or any step's refusal refuses the whole instance and publishes nothing from it; a published version is immutable within a project. Instances are created, edited and moved to a new version in the history text (the app's scripting surface) and through the `add_custom_feature` patch operation. Regression evidence: `packages/scripting/src/script.test.ts` (grammar and diagnostics, SHA-256 vectors, exact query semantics, interpreter), `test/custom-feature.test.ts` (a rounded, shelled tray matching its closed-form volume, picks re-run after a parameter edit, count, digest, input and republish refusals, creation and version bumps through the text, script errors at their line), `test/e2e/history-text-custom-feature.spec.ts` (`@ci-smoke`: add, edit, refuse, undo, redo and reload in the browser).

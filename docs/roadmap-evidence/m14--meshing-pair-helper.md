Meshing-pair helper (no schema change): Assemble's Gears button (shown once two parts are gears) opens Mesh gears, which takes the gear that stays put and the one that meshes with it, shows the working centre distance, working pressure angle, turns per base turn (rack: travel per turn) and backlash, and Mesh saves an ordinary revolute joint on the second gear's own axis whose value 0 is the meshed pose (a floating base is fixed first, as Set up motion does). Covers external spur and helical pairs (equal module, pressure angle and helix angle, opposite hands; herringbone with herringbone), a pinion inside an internal gear (same hand) and a spur pinion on a rack. The centre distance is the working one with profile shift (ISO 21771 `inv α_wt = inv α_t + 2 tan α_n Σx / Σz`, internal pairs by `x₂ − x₁` over `z₂ − z₁`; a pinion stands `r + x·m` off the rack's pitch line); backlash is the gears' own tooth thinning, so the centres stay at the zero-backlash distance and the play is split either side of each tooth. The tooth phase follows the kernel's tooth placement (tooth 0 on the x direction turned by phase, helical teeth turning `h·tan β / r` by height, rack teeth at `(k + ½)·p`) with mid-face planes level. Refused by code (`gear-mesh:*`): bevel and worm pairs (intersecting or crossed axes, not placed yet), two racks, two internal gears, a rack with an internal or helical gear, mismatched module, pressure angle or helix angle, same-hand external or opposite-hand internal helical pairs, herringbone with single helix, a ring with no more teeth than its pinion, shifts that leave no working pressure angle, teeth cut into a cylinder, a gear moved after it was made, and parts that are not gears. Gear-ratio coupling between joints and partial-span teeth remain. Evidence: `test/gear-mesh.test.ts` (exact centre distances and working pressure angles for standard, shifted, helical, internal and rack pairs; phase interleaving at arbitrary phases, hands and orientations; every refusal code; document resolution in inch units and the moved-gear refusal; the saved joint landing the wheel at the helper's frame and turning it in place; and on the exact kernel, grids of points across the pitch point showing spur, helical, internal and rack pairs built where the helper puts them never overlap, while a turn past the play, half a pitch or the wrong hand does), `apps/web/src/components/assembly/GearMeshCard.test.tsx` and `test/e2e/gear-mesh.spec.ts` (Gears opens the card at 60 mm with 0.2 mm play, Mesh fixes the pinion and adds Gear mesh, one undo removes it).

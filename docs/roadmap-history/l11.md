## Outcome and completion evidence

End-to-end encrypted project data: the browser encrypts documents, revisions, imported sources, names, thumbnails, measurements, view state and archived exports, so the Worker, D1, R2 and the collaboration room hold only ciphertext and act as blob store, access control and relay.
Design and settled product decisions: [ADR-024](../../docs/adrs/ADR-024-end-to-end-encrypted-projects.md) (opt-in per account during the beta, assistant asks per project, names encrypted, no escrow, off switch beta-only, dormant accounts encrypt on next sign-in).
Delivered:
Phase 0, the ADR ([PR #743](https://github.com/esaueng/OpenZCAD/pull/743)), and Phase 1a, client-minted projects and save points ([PR #745](https://github.com/esaueng/OpenZCAD/pull/745)).
Group key management uses MLS (RFC 9420) through `mls-rs` 0.56 with the RustCrypto provider, pinned exactly (decided 2026-10-19 on the spike in [PR #748](https://github.com/esaueng/OpenZCAD/pull/748)); the custom roster and epoch protocol is superseded, and the Phase 8 outside review covers the MLS integration and the pinned version.

The consent copy lists everything an assistant request sends (`apps/web/src/lib/assistant/consentCopy.ts`): the prompt, up to 12 earlier conversation turns, the model digest (project id, name, units, version, schema version, parameters, every feature's full definition including sketch text, bodies with volumes, bounding boxes and measured faces and edges, the measured edit catalog, warnings) and the selection, attached images and drawing pages with their file names, the client's operation list and a retry flag; it names the provider route (the user's own key, or the deployment's provider and model, and the endpoint's host), says that OpenZCAD's server reads the request in plaintext to pass it on with its own instructions, any custom instructions and an account-derived identifier, and that what was sent cannot be recalled. `ASSISTANT_CONSENT_COPY_VERSION` (1) is pinned to the exact request and digest field lists by tests, including a compile-time map of `CadDocumentDigest`'s keys.

Before, revoking a device (`POST /api/e2ee/device-list`) and storing its statement (`PUT /api/e2ee/revocation-cutoffs`) were two requests, and a browser that went offline in between left a revoked device without a statement, which co-members could not tell from a device revoked before the account joined their project, so they kept writing under a key the removed device may still derive.

Phase 4, slice 4-6, encrypted content blobs with HMAC dedupe: D1 migration `0034_e2ee_content_blobs.sql` adds `e2ee_content_blobs` (per project and blob id: kind, opaque object key `e2ee/<projectId>/<blobId>`, ciphertext bytes, an optional dedupe tag unique per project, and a committed or deleting state), `e2ee_version_blobs` (the blob ids each accepted version's header names, the reference count garbage collection reads) and an `e2ee_blob_kind` marker on `upload_sessions`, with triggers that refuse a reference to an uncommitted blob, a claim on a blob a retained version names, the removal of an unclaimed blob, any change to what the Worker accepted, and an encrypted session becoming an artifact; blobs share the artifact quota and reservation accounting of migrations 0017 and 0022, counting ciphertext, and carry erasure fences. `apps/web/worker/e2eeContentBlobs.ts` serves `/api/e2ee/projects/:id/blobs` (`GET ?tag=`), `blobs/uploads` (POST: an upload session for the blob id the browser proposes, the same one on a retry, or the committed blob), `blobs/:blobId/finalize` (POST) and `blobs/:blobId` (GET ciphertext), behind the key gate, `E2EE_PROJECTS_ENABLED`, migrations 0031–0034 (503 without them), the session/erasure write predicate, owner only and format 2 only; the bytes go up through the ordinary single-part and multipart upload routes, and finalize reads only the stored object's head (`parseEnvelopeShape`, new in `@openzcad/crypto`) to require a content blob of the session's kind named by the session's id, within its kind's ceiling, exactly `envelopeBodyLength` long and, uncompressed, its whole declared size, discarding a refused object with its session.

## Outcome and completion evidence

Quiet-stage workspace redesign, decided 2026-09-23: the viewport owns the screen, four islands float over it, and only the left command card changes with the selection (one primary verb per context); right-hand icon instruments with Items/History/Parameters in one drawer (superseded by [U08](../../ROADMAP.md#u08)'s dockable model panels); a hint line and a ⌘K bar that also asks the assistant; a fixed sketch relations rail; the "B refined" face-offset preview.
Seven slices, one PR each, recorded here as they merge.
Slice 1, visual foundation: palette A (steel neutrals, one `#6798ff` signal, add `#48cd8f`, cut `#ff644d`, magenta construction), Geist Sans/Mono replacing IBM Plex, a near-square radius scale (2/3/4 px), raw component-CSS colours mapped onto the palette families and the viewport blues shifted to cobalt on a steel stage, with no layout change (#411; `theme/contrast.test.ts`, `test/css-class-coverage.test.ts`).
Slice 2, shell islands: the top bar floats as three islands (identity, mode switch, actions) over a full-height viewport; the viewer bar is a vertical instrument rail top-right; filter/snap/log form a readout under the column; the scale bar sits beside the cube; the bottom lane is the workspace prompt over a ⌘K search bar that replaces the column's search button (#412; `quiet-stage.css`, `WorkspaceReadout.test.tsx`, `sidebar-divider.spec.ts`, `viewport-overlay-lanes.spec.ts`, `settings.spec.ts`).
Slice 3, command card: the tool palette becomes one card whose header names the pick and whose rows are the tools for that kind of pick (idle, one body, several bodies, face, edges, sketch region), one primary tool marked, every other tool in an always-visible icon grid under the same accessible names; the face/edge verbs stay in the floating tool card so nothing appears twice (#413; `lib/commandContext.test.ts`, `CommandCard.test.tsx`).
Slice 4, model drawer: parameters, bodies, history, revisions and diagnostics leave the column for a drawer beside the instrument rail, closed by default and opened on the named section by the rail's Items, History and Parameters buttons (the pressed one closes it); it stops above the cube and scale and the column hugs the command card; the inspector and the drawer share one right lane (inspector on top, drawer filling the rest), so neither pushes into the canvas, while direct-edit controls and the rail's panels float over the lane (#414; `test/panel-state.test.ts`, `test/e2e/quiet-stage-drawer.spec.ts`; the e2e fixtures seed the drawer open for specs that exercise its contents).

Slice 7, face-offset preview ("B refined"): only the change is coloured — the pin stands on the outer end of the change (the new face adding, the old level cutting), a solid white change arrow runs between the two levels on its axis from a dashed ring at the old level (coral when cutting), the face's boundary loops sweep into a hatched band of the walls it adds (green, crisp seam at the old level) or removes (coral, with the removed slab outlined in dashed coral), and the label sits beside the pin; moving a face reads Offset by default while resizing a primitive keeps Total (this PR; `rigs.test.ts`; `data-e2e-offset-dimension-visible` became `data-e2e-offset-change-visible`; the moved face keeps its selection fill for now, since fading it slowed hosted CI's software-GL drags past their budgets, and a neutral face is a follow-up).

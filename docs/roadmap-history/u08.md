## Outcome and completion evidence

Dockable model panels, decided 2026-10-20 on a design canvas:
Parameters, Objects, History, Revisions and Diagnostics leave U04's single drawer and become independent panels that open unpinned from the rail, pin into stacked columns docked at either screen edge (rails on the inner edge, up to three columns right and two left), or float as movable, resizable, stackable windows; dragging a header or a rail icon previews the landing slot; a View options menu holds presets (Essentials default), Hide all, Lock, Compact and Width; layouts persist per device.
Six slices, one PR each: layout model and persistence, panel extraction, right dock replacing the drawer (with the v1 migration, the no-dock rule at 720 px and below and the e2e fixture and count-readout migration), drag/left dock/floating (with the combined width cap), view options, documentation.
Slice 1, layout model: pure `lib/panelLayout.ts` with placement, drag preview, stacking weights, floating z-order, recency, presets, view options, the 60% dock cap with parking, the 720 px no-dock rule, geometry and hit-testing, and the `openzcad-panel-layout:v1` load/save with v1 migration; nothing renders it yet (#766; `test/panel-layout.test.ts`).
Slice 2, extraction: `components/modelPanels/` holds Parameters, Objects, History, Revisions and Diagnostics as standalone panels with their counts and hooks, and `Sidebar` composes them with an unchanged drawer DOM (#767; `modelPanels/*.test.tsx`).
Slice 3, right dock: `PanelDock` replaces the drawer and `Sidebar.tsx`; a rail press opens a panel unpinned beside the rail, Pin docks it into one stacked right column with gap resizing and an even split, Close remembers its home, Revisions and Diagnostics join the rail, sketch and model drags suspend every open panel, ⌘K presents History or Parameters, nothing docks at 720 px and below (one panel in the right lane), and the layout key migrates from `openzcad-panel-state:v1` on first load (#771; `test/e2e/panel-dock.spec.ts`, which replaces `quiet-stage-drawer.spec.ts` and `drawer-disclosure-targets.spec.ts`).
Slice 4, drag, left dock and floating: a header or rail drag-out lifts a panel to a chip beside the cursor and previews its slot in a column, a new column past either rail (the rail sliding over while hovered) or a window on the model, the emptied column holding until the drop; floating windows stack, move by header or frame grip, resize from the corner and keep a stored z-order a press, focus, rail press or ⌘K raises; the grip's Move menu, Alt+↑/↓, the gaps' arrow keys and the windows' move and resize keys do the same by keyboard, and Pop out joins the header above 720 px; the left dock publishes `--left-dock-w`, which the verb rail and the bottom lanes step past; the 60% cap spans both docks; new devices start in the two-sided Essentials; drags commit once and the viewer does not re-render during one (#788; `test/e2e/panel-drag.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/panel-float.spec.ts`).
Slice 5, view options: the panel rail's View options menu (there was no Layout button to replace) picks Essentials, Full workspace, Modeling, Review or Model only, keeping the settings and turning Hide off, and marks the preset the layout still matches;
Hide all keeps the layout behind a "Panels hidden · Show" pill and toggles on `Ctrl+\` (`⌘\`), a global, remappable `lib/keymap.ts` command;
Lock layout turns off header and rail drags, gap and window resizing, the Move menu's placements and the move and resize keys, and disables the presets and Panel width with "Unlock the layout to change it" while Pin, Pop out and Close still act;
Compact rows tightens every panel's rows;
Panel width S/M/L (256/296/360 px) re-runs the cap, so Large parks Essentials' left column at 1280 px; all of it persists in `openzcad-panel-layout:v1` with no format change, and the menu's radio and checkbox items take the arrow keys (#789; `test/e2e/panel-view-options.spec.ts`).
Slice 6, documentation: the quiet-stage plan marks its slice 4 drawer superseded and U04's row points here; `README.md` and `docs/design/README.md` describe the panels as built — a rail press opens one unpinned, Pin docks it, Close remembers its home, a header or rail drag stacks it in either dock, a new column or a floating window, the Move menu and keys do the same, View options holds the presets (Essentials by default), Hide all (`Ctrl+\`, `⌘\` on macOS), Lock, Compact and Width S/M/L, a stage 720 px wide or less shows one panel at a time in the phone lane, and `openzcad-panel-layout:v1` keeps the layout per device; the README's Build capture is regenerated in place and two new captures show a stacked floating window and the open View options menu, all three 1600 × 1000 from the production bundle with anonymous API fixtures, written by the opt-in `test/e2e/docs-captures.spec.ts` (skipped unless `OZ_CAPTURE=1`, weighted 0 in `shard-weights.json`) (#790; local captures, documentation evidence only; `test/deploy-safety.test.ts`, `test/e2e-shards.test.ts`).
All six slices are recorded; production acceptance is separate.
Docked columns fill the dock by default again (Fit panels to content is opt-in, stored as `hug` so the old default-on `fit` key no longer applies); a handle under each docked column sets its height (drag, arrow keys, End or a double-click to fill), remembered per set of panels, so a second panel in a shortened column fills the dock and taking it out restores the height (`panel-layout.test.ts`, `panel-dock.spec.ts`).
Full-height docks and denser panels: the docks run from under the top bar to the window's foot, the cube moves to the model's bottom-right inside the instrument rail, and the scale bar stands upright against the model's left edge; floating windows may go anywhere on the stage, over the top bar and rails too; docked panels hug their content (View options › Fit panels to content, on by default) with a 4px dock gap between panels; the selection filter chip is gone (Q cycles and hands back to the tool, the status line names each choice); shorter panel headers, one-row Objects find/filter, and parameter descriptions shown only while written or being edited (`panel-layout.test.ts`, `sidebarContracts.test.ts`, `column-panel-sizes.test.ts`, `ViewportScaleIndicator.test.tsx`, panel and viewport e2e specs; PR #845).

## Outcome and completion evidence

In-viewport commands, decided 2026-10-24 ([plan](../../docs/plans/in-viewport-tools-plan-2026-10-24.md)): every Build tool runs as picks in the model, an evaluated preview and handles with keypad chips, finished by a Done/Cancel pill; the feature form goes away (rare options behind an Options popover; docked model panels unchanged), the selection card offers every applicable tool while the rail stays fixed, primitives place by click-drag and Scale pivots on the bounding-box centre.
Phase 1 proves the contract on Revolve (profile, then an edge or axis, angle rig); each converted tool needs an e2e spec driven only by picks, drags and the keypad, and the same command factory as the AI patch path.
Axes and directions are picked in the model, as the plan's §5 table has them.
Revolve, Rotate about axis and Circular pattern take a straight edge or a cylinder's (or cone's) axis besides the listed lines, and Rotate about axis now lists X, Y and Z too (Revolve a world axis in its sketch plane that is not its own); the stored features only turn about datum axes, so Done adds the picked line as a datum axis in the same undoable step, just before the feature (an edit moves it before the edited one), and the turn follows its source. A revolve refuses a line off its profile's plane before the build would. A linear pattern reads a straight edge, a flat face's normal or a datum axis as its repeat direction (stored as its numbers, a world axis when it is one).
Circular pattern adds Equal spacing / Fixed step under Options, a fixed step storing the total its copies span.
Not expressible without schema work, so left out: a two-sided revolve (its angle runs one way from the profile, (0°, 360°]) and a sweep along model edges (its path is sketch curves only) (`tools.test.ts`, `datumPicks.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, `in-place-axis-picks.spec.ts`, `datum-axes.spec.ts`).
Extrude runs in place: a region pick (or E) arms the region's arrow as before, and the extrude card's form is gone. A second arrow out of the back face sets an independent back distance, a rig hinged on the foot of the profile's longest side sets the draft angle, and each chip opens the keypad.
The card's settings moved under the bar's Options with the same refusals (`lib/extrudeDraft.ts`, shared with the History editor): operation (Automatic, New body, Add, Cut, Intersect) and its target body, extent (Distance, To face with its face pick, Through all on Cut with its direction), One side or Symmetric, Distance, Back distance and Draft angle typed as expressions, and Reverse direction;
Done or Enter creates it.
History edits of an extrude still open its editor card (`extrudeDraft.test.ts`, `inPlace/extrude.test.ts`, `ExtrudeCommand.test.tsx`, `extrude-in-place.spec.ts`, `extrude-intent.spec.ts`, `extrude-to-face-draft.spec.ts`, `extrude-through-all.spec.ts`, `multi-region-extrude.spec.ts`).
Phase 7:
Fillet and Chamfer run in place and their card is gone.
Edges are clicks (in pick order, one body; the selection, a context menu or Options' Select all fill them), the size is a linear handle on the first edge with its chip, and the card's other inputs moved under the bar's Options:
Radius change (constant, linear, eased) with an End R chip, a chamfer's shape (equal, distance and angle, two distances) with its chip and Swap the two faces, and the edges listed by name with remove buttons.
Done commits through the card's own `edgeModifierCommand`, and a History row re-arms the same command, edges resolved by their stored names (`tools.test.ts`, `edit.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, `InPlaceCommandBar.test.tsx`, `edge-form-preview.spec.ts`, `edit-card-lifecycle.spec.ts`, `build-reach.spec.ts` F30, `hook-all-edge-fillet.spec.ts`).
Phase 7: a History row re-arms the in-place command that made its feature (revolve, loft, sweep, helical sweep, hole, shell, thicken, solid offset, draft, mirror, split, scale, rotate about axis, the booleans and the patterns) with its picks in the slots, its values on the chips and its choices in Options, instead of opening a form.
What the cards typed is typed on chips, expressions kept: a mirror or split plane, a helix axis, a draft's pull and hinge, a hole's U and V, a scale's pivot, a loft apex, a pattern's vector, so an untouched Done writes the feature back unchanged.
Values and options the user has not changed follow the feature when someone else changes it.
Done commits the same `updateFeature` the cards did, validated the same way. A Move still edits in its card (`edit.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, `test/e2e/in-place-history-edit.spec.ts`, `edit-card-lifecycle.spec.ts`).
Phase 7: an in-place Hole's counterbore or countersink is sized on chips (its diameter and depth, or diameter and included angle) beside the bore.
Until a chip is set the step follows the bore with the card's old ratios; a typed value or expression is stored as typed (`tools.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, two `in-place-face-tools.spec.ts` specs that read the exported hole).
Phases 2–5:
Loft, Sweep, Helical sweep, Hole, Shell, Thicken, Solid offset, Draft, Mirror, Split, Scale, Rotate about axis, Union, Subtract, Intersect and the linear, circular and grid patterns run in place through one command table (`apps/web/src/lib/inPlace/tools.ts`) and one hook (`apps/web/src/hooks/useInPlaceCommand.ts`).
Picks are region, curve, face, body and plane clicks (a box sweep fills Shell and Draft); numbers are linear, angle and chip handles with keypad entry; rare choices sit behind the bar's Options menu;
Done waits for an exact preview of exactly what it commits (`tools.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, `linearRig.test.ts`, `curvePickerRig.test.ts`, `test/e2e/in-place-sketch-tools.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/in-place-face-tools.spec.ts`).
Primitives, Plane, Axis, Image and Fillet/Chamfer still open their cards.
Phase 6 (P, R):
Plane, Axis and Image run in place. A Plane's sources are picked in the model — one plane (a ghost principal plane, a datum or a flat face) is an offset plane with an offset handle, two a midplane, a plane and an edge end a parallel plane through it, three edge ends a plane through them — each kept as the associative source the card stored;
Flip normal is in Options.
An Axis is inferred the same way from a straight edge, a cylinder, two edge ends or two planes, with Reverse in Options.
The datum is previewed from the exact build before Done, which commits through the same resolved-datum check the card ran.
An Image asks for its picture, then a plane or face; a click places it on the sketch snap, a corner handle scales it, a ring turns it, an offset chip slides it along a principal plane's normal, two clicks on it and a distance chip scale it to size, and opacity is in Options;
Done adds it as one step and Cancel releases its bytes (`datums.test.ts`, `image.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, `test/e2e/datum-planes.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/datum-point-planes.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/datum-axes.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/reference-images.spec.ts`, `test/e2e/datum-plane-viewport.spec.ts`).
Typed point positions and 5% opacity steps stay on the History and image cards.
Phase 6, primitives in place:
Box, Cylinder, Cone, Sphere and Torus open no card.
The command previews the card's default at the origin at once, so Enter alone still makes it; a press-drag on the ground (or the upright principal plane facing an edge-on view) or on a flat face draws the footprint, a box's rectangle corner to corner and a round primitive's circle from its centre; the sizes are linear handles with chips, the height an arrow off the plane; the exact position (corner, base center or center) is typed from the bar's Options.
On a face square to an axis a box stays unturned; a round primitive on a side face, or anything on a tilted face, turns in the same placement Move.
Commits go through the card's own `createPrimitiveCommand` (`primitives.test.ts`, `tools.test.ts`, `useInPlaceCommand.test.tsx`, `InPlaceCommandBar.test.tsx`, `primitivePlacement.test.ts`, `test/e2e/in-place-primitives.spec.ts`; the e2e suite places primitives through `createPrimitiveInPlace`).
Editing a primitive from History still opens its card.
Phase 1, Revolve in place: no card; the profile is a region click, the axis a click on one of the drawn sketch or datum axis lines, the angle a drag of the new angle rig (`packages/viewport/src/gizmo/angleRig.ts`) or its chip's keypad, with an exact preview through the commit's own command and a Cancel / Done bar in the guidance row.
Schema v38 adds the revolve's region `profile`, so a rectangle drawn as four edges revolves (`test/region-revolve.test.ts`, `useInPlaceRevolve.test.tsx`, `angleRig.test.ts`, `axisPickerRig.test.ts`, `InPlaceCommandBar.test.tsx`, `test/e2e/in-place-revolve.spec.ts`).
Gap closure (options and rigs):
Move finishes on the in-place Cancel / Done bar in the command row instead of a card in the right lane, and its six exact numbers, name, body picker and Copy sit behind the bar's Options; the gizmo is unchanged.
Draft's angle is an angle rig hinged where the first face meets the neutral plane (base, top, clicked point, origin or typed), its arm up the face so a positive drag leans the face out of the body as a positive draft does; a curved face or one square to the pull keeps the typed chip.
Not expressible on the pinned kernel and schema, so not offered: a left-handed helix (the kernel refuses a negative pitch, "pitch must be positive", and reversing the axis keeps a right-handed coil) and an outward shell ("Shell thickness must be finite and positive at the solid's scale"; the stored thickness is inward only) (`tools.test.ts`, `DirectModelingOverlays.test.tsx`, `TransformForm.test.tsx`, `in-place-face-tools.spec.ts`, `panel-dock.spec.ts`, the Move specs).
Phase 7: the form cards the in-place commands replaced are gone.
Every modeling tool except Move, Extrude, the primitives, sketches, datums and reference images starts and edits in place, so the Revolve, Boolean, Pattern, Fillet/Chamfer and modeling-operation (Mirror, Split, Hole, Shell, Thicken, Solid offset, Draft) cards, the Inspector branches and App state that fed them, and the Hole card's bore ghost were unreachable and are deleted (about 7,500 lines).
The commits they made still go through the same `updateFeature` and feature commands the in-place Done uses; the Move card's Transform form stays for the Move edits it serves.
Phase 7: the Selection card carries the verbs the plan's §5 table gives a pick. A flat face adds Shell, Draft, Thicken and Plane after its own edits, Sketch and Hole; a round face Axis and Shell; edges Axis; a body Rotate, Scale, Split, Pattern and Solid offset beside Move and Mirror; two bodies Subtract and Intersect beside Union and Move.
The card shows the first four as buttons, so a pick's usual verbs keep their places, and the rest behind a More button in its header; a lit verb is always a button. A picked region's Extrude card offers Revolve, Sweep and Loft (the region as its first section), each starting its in-place command with the region already in its slot (`selectionCallout.test.ts`, `SelectionCard.test.tsx`, `ToolCard.test.tsx`, `in-place-face-tools.spec.ts`, `in-place-sketch-tools.spec.ts`).
Done 2026-10-26: every Build tool runs in place, merged in #866, #867, #873, #883, #885, #887, #889, #893, #895, #896, #917, #919, #920 and #934.
What §5 asks for and the pinned kernel or schema cannot express is left out by name, not approximated: a two-sided revolve, a sweep along model edges, a left-handed helix and an outward shell.
Extrude edits run in place too, and the extrude card is gone: a History row (or the Inspector's Edit extrude) re-arms the in-place Extrude seeded from the stored feature, expressions as written, with its own distance arrow, the back arrow and the draft rig on the model and its extent (to-face target and through-all side included), Sides and stored operation and target under the bar's Options; an edit never infers an operation.
Done commits through the same `updateFeature` the card's Apply used, validated and refused the same way (its refusals said in the bar), and an untouched Done writes the feature back exactly as stored (`extrudeEditData`). `ExtrudeForm`, `ExtrudeExtentControls`, `ExtrudeControls` and the Inspector's extrude branch are deleted (`extrudeEditing.test.ts`, `ExtrudeCommand.test.tsx`, `extrude-edit-in-place.spec.ts`, `extrude-intent.spec.ts`, `extrude-through-all.spec.ts`, `extrude-to-face-draft.spec.ts`, `feature-history.spec.ts`, `solid-result-options.spec.ts`).
